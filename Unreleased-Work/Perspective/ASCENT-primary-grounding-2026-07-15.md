## §2 — Gebser's structures = the evolution of the APERTURE (★ CONFIRMED sharpening — this is §2's spine)

Gebser's five structures of consciousness are organized by **degree of perspectivity** — *unperspectival → pre-perspectival → perspectival → aperspectival.* That axis **is our keyhole/aperture language.** The "evolution of human attention across the eras" (Clayton's ask) is precisely **the evolution of the perspectival structure of the aperture** — each era a different *settledness-profile* of the one config space ([[LC60]]: realm = subspace; individuation = re-profiling). Not "different beliefs," but different *grains of the keyhole itself.*

- **Archaic** — near-zero perspectivity; full identity with world; no separation (the pre-aperture; the plenum barely narrowed).
- **Magical** — **one-dimensional, pre-perspectival, spaceless/timeless, participatory, emotion/instinct-dominated; logic = sympathy/antipathy (like affects like).** Egoless; a partial differentiation out of archaic non-differentiation.
- **Mythical** — (to deepen) two-dimensional, polar, imaginal; the world spoken in image and myth; the soul discovered.
- **Mental-rational** — perspectival, three-dimensional; the ego fixed in space, the detached observer; *this is the water modernity swims in* (and its deficient mode = the disenchantment Weber named — the numinous banished by the iron cage).
- **Integral** — aperspectival; all prior structures held transparently, "diaphanous"; not a return to magic but the whole spectrum made available.

**★ The §3 decode this hands us (non-credulous, structural):** magic's core logic — *sympathy/antipathy, action at a distance, like-affects-like* — is **the phenomenology of a low-perspectival, high-participation aperture**, one that has not yet differentiated self from world. When the keyhole's grain is coarse (self/world boundary thin), *registration-is-participation is felt as literal causal connection* — because at that settledness-profile it nearly is, *for that navigator's world.* This decodes the magical worldview **through the framework** without endorsing literal sympathetic causation: the magician is operating an aperture at a participatory settledness-profile, and the "magic" is the real phenomenology of that profile. (Ties the ascent's §3 to malheur's mechanism: same reality-tunnel substrate, different grain.)

**Grade:** Gebser = received/interpretive (a phenomenology of history, not a measured science — flag as such); the *perspectivity-axis ≅ aperture-grain* mapping is our decode (framework-use, well-motivated). The magical-participation decode is SOLID as a structural reading, SPECULATIVE if pushed to "therefore sympathetic magic works" (don't push it — that's the credulity trap; hold at phenomenology).

**FALSIFY-watch result so far:** no tension exposed yet; the mapping is cleaner than expected (perspectivity was *already* our axis). Watch the mythical structure when deepened — "two-dimensional/polar" may or may not map to our bipolar-gravity cleanly.

## §3 — Crowley's theorems: magick = intentional navigation with understanding (★ CONFIRMED-AND-SHARPENED)

The actual theorems of *Magick in Theory and Practice* (Book 4, Part III) are far stronger for the decode than the one-line definition. Verbatim/near-verbatim:
- **Definition:** "Magick is the Science and Art of causing Change to occur in conformity with Will." + "Magick is the Science of understanding oneself and one's conditions. It is the Art of applying that understanding in action."
- **Theorem: "Every intentional act is a Magical Act."** (By "intentional" = "willed.") "Every successful act has conformed to the postulate. Every failure proves that one or more requirements of the postulate have not been fulfilled."
- **The central postulate:** "ANY required change may be effected by the application of the proper kind and degree of Force in the proper manner, through the proper medium to the proper object."
- **Requisites:** (1) "thorough qualitative and quantitative understanding of the conditions"; (2) "the practical ability to set in right motion the necessary forces."
- **Theorem:** "Every human being is intrinsically an independent individual with his own proper character and proper motion."

**★ The decode (this is §3's Crowley body):**
1. **"Every intentional act is a Magical Act" = every act of navigation shapes the reality-tunnel.** Crowley *collapses* the special category "magic" into willed action as such — which is our exact move: there is no supernatural magic, there is navigation (will directing configuration-change), and "magick" is the name for doing it *deliberately, with understanding.* This DEMYSTIFIES magic in the framework's own way — the magician's real claim is not "I break physics" but "all intentional action already shapes one's conditions; magick is doing it skillfully."
2. **Magick = "understanding oneself and one's conditions" + the first requisite = measure before you shape** ([[LC59]]: measure the boundary; don't trust the un-measured). The magician is a navigator who insists on knowing the terrain first. This is the scientist half *inside* the occult definition.
3. **"Every failure proves a requirement unfulfilled" = a falsifiable, no-excuses stance** — startlingly scientific for an occultist, and the textual vindication of the scientist-magus (Dee → Crowley) reading. Magick as Crowley defined it is *epistemically humble about its own failures.*
4. **"Proper motion" = True Will = the intrinsic trajectory = Heaven-as-navigational-state** (Part VI already defines heaven as "the path of one's own coherence"). True Will = navigating along one's proper motion; hell = maximal distance from it. Crowley's "proper motion" and the book's "own coherence" are the same object.

**★ FALSIFY / sharpening (the drive's real catch — corrects my Day-164 framing):** I had filed Crowley as having "already half-corrected himself" (whim → True Will), needing only our co-constitutive completion. The primary source shows the completion has **TWO axes, not one**, and Crowley moved *opposite* directions on them:
- On the **will**: he climbed (ego-whim → True Will = the sacred proper-motion). ✓ our ally here.
- On the **self**: he *entrenched* the error — "intrinsically an **independent** individual" — explicitly positing the self-standing aperture our co-constitutivism denies. He did NOT half-correct this; he doubled down.
So the honest completion is: *keep Crowley's proper-motion (True Will is real — it's the intrinsic trajectory), correct his "independent" (the individual is co-constituted, its proper motion drawn through the same relations it would command).* "Do what thou art permitted" corrects the **independence**, not the Will. This is sharper and more honest than "he half-agreed" — and it means §3 should stage Crowley as **right about the Will, wrong about the standing**, which is a cleaner, more defensible decode than the Day-164 version.

**Resonance:** "every intentional act is magical" ≅ **registration-is-participation** (every willed attention shapes the shared field); Crowley universalized magic to all intentional action exactly as we universalize reality-shaping to all navigation.

**Grade:** Crowley's definitions = primary/received (his system, sincerely stated). The navigation↔magick mapping = our decode (framework-use, well-motivated, now textually anchored). Do NOT import Crowley's cosmology (Aiwass, the Aeons, the God-forms) — take the *operational definition* (magick = skilled intentional navigation) and leave the metaphysics filed as his smuggle ([[LC62]]).

**Prediction outcome:** CONFIRMED (theorems map more precisely than the one-liner) AND the FALSIFY-watch fired usefully (the "independent individual" tension corrected my earlier one-axis completion → two-axis).
