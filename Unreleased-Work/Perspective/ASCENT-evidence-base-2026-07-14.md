## 5. The graded evidence base

### 〔MAINSTREAM SCIENCE — the keystone〕 Predictive processing
Standard cognitive neuroscience (Seth, Friston, Hohwy). Perception is not passive reception: the brain generates hypotheses and tests them against sensory input. Seth — "controlled hallucination": what you perceive is your brain's best *prediction*, constrained (not dictated) by the senses. **The "reality-tunnel" is the standard model of perception, not a mystical claim.** Priors constitute the perceived world; change the priors, change the world experienced.
- **Framework connection:** empirical confirmation of the book's Part I (the aperture constitutes the world). "Different conditioning in different eras produced different realities" = predictive processing at cultural scale (culture installs priors; priors constitute perception). Magic/ritual, decoded = **deliberate prior-restructuring** → reshapes the controlled hallucination → via registration-as-participation, reshapes the enacted world. The occult "reality-tunnel" and Seth's "controlled hallucination" are the SAME claim in two vocabularies. Also: Lush/Dienes/Seth on *phenomenological control* — expectancy generating perceptual experience — grounds suggestion/ritual/placebo as real mechanisms.
- **Grade: measured / converging.** This is the respectability spine of the whole ascent.

### 〔CONCEPTUAL / PRIMARY — and the sharpest find〕 Aleister Crowley
Magick = "the science and art of causing change to occur in conformity with Will" — startlingly close to our navigation-as-will-shaping-configuration (science/art/will → systematic/craft/directed).
- **★ The completion (sharper than rejection):** "Do what thou wilt" was NEVER hedonism for Crowley — he distinguished *True Will* (the sacred inner calling, "responsibility and hard work") from ego-whim. So Crowley *already half-corrected himself* — climbed from whim to True Will — but stopped at the aperture's own interior, locating the will in "a sacred inner core of personal divinity," self-standing. **Our correction (= Clayton's "Do what thou art permitted, or dissolve") completes the ladder he was on:** even True Will is *co-constituted*, drawn through the same relations it would command, not sourced from a private divine core. He got one rung up; we finish the climb. Honors him as a real detector; corrects only the last step.
- **Grade: received (conceptual/cultural data).** Thelema was a real detection with the sign flipped on the will's source.

### 〔DOCUMENTED + PRIMARY — our ancestor〕 John Dee (1527–1608)
One of the leading *mathematicians* of Elizabethan England, cartographer, imperial navigator, cryptographer, Elizabeth's court advisor — AND the most ambitious occultist of his age (seven years scrying Enochian with Edward Kelley). Largest private library in England (~4,000 vols).
- **★ The seat:** Dee is the historical proof that the scientist-magus seat is real and was *institutionally central*. Our ancestor down to the method: his *Monas Hieroglyphica* (1564) tried to compress "the unity underlying the whole universe into a single hieroglyph" — the **Monad**, the same zero-point that centered the magic circle (§6). Dee spent his life encoding the cosmos in one generative symbol; we're doing that with the Coherence Principle.
- **Grade split:** the mathematics/navigation = documented history; the angelic conversations = sincere primary data, mechanism open (Kelley may have partly conned him). As evidence of the seat and the technology of attention: priceless.

### 〔CONTESTED — file open, honestly〕 Remote viewing / Project Stargate
NOT "debunked." Utts (statistics professor) and Hyman (psychologist-skeptic) evaluated the *same* government data (SAIC, ganzfeld) and BOTH acknowledged a statistically significant anomaly the usual flaws didn't explain — then split on meaning: Utts said real and replicated; Hyman said real-looking but "too erratic to rely on" and lacking a mechanism. A genuine live contest between experts.
- **Framework connection:** Hyman's own objection — *erratic* — is exactly what the butterfly-effect / sensitive-dependence framing predicts: real influence, unsteerable in detail. The perfect specimen for the ascent's method, because the honest verdict IS "contested, unresolved," and saying so cleanly is the point.
- **Grade: contested / staked-open.** Real 20-year government program; real statistical signal both reviewers granted; unresolved on mechanism and reliability.

### 〔DOCUMENTED — the dark anchor〕 MKUltra
Not fringe — archive. Church Committee 1975; 1977 FOIA surfaced ~20,000 surviving documents (most destroyed on Helms's 1973 order) → Senate hearings. Real CIA program 1953–64: drugs + behavioral modification, the state trying to weaponize the shaping of perception. Congressional finding: *"prior consent was obviously not obtained from any of the subjects."*
- **★ Framework connection (via Clayton's catechism):** "Consent is the Law; lack thereof, the destruction of the Self." MKUltra is the documented, archival proof-case: perception-shaping run **over** its subjects, no blessing asked, real selves destroyed. It makes the ascent undeniable — the reality-tunnel is real enough that a government spent a decade trying to hijack it, and *through-vs-over* is not abstract when the archive shows what *over* did to real people. Also validates the book's own "making of minds" / coercion-at-the-substrate material.
- **Grade: measured / documented.**

### 〔SPLIT GRADE — the honest-grading exemplar〕 Subliminal / the subconscious

So "the subconscious shapes perception" is true AND requires saying which part: semantic — solid; social-goal — shaky; subliminal-advertising-runs-your-life — myth.

### 〔MYTH / ARCHETYPE — the third seat mythologized〕 Merlin
Myrddin → Merlin: amalgam of Myrddin Wyllt (wild man/bard of the forest), Ambrosius the warrior-prophet, probably a pagan god; crystallized by Geoffrey of Monmouth. The archetype: "the wise magician who bridges the mystical and mortal worlds," "the tension between order and chaos, civilization and wilderness, knowledge and mystery" — the wild prophet AND the king's rational advisor at once; sorcerer, seer, shapeshifter.
