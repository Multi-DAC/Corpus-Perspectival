## The discipline — two tests, run on every cross-domain term before it stays on the page

**1. The strip test (Fable / Woan-Bayley / Kato).** Remove the borrowed word; substitute plain, domain-native language. Ask: *does a specific, checkable claim survive?*
- **Survives** → the borrowed word was compression of a real structure. Keep it; the structure is the content.
- **Nothing survives** except a vaguer restatement → the word was **authority-borrowing** (Kato's "mathematics speaks"; Sokal's Gödel). **Cut it, or demote it to a declared metaphor** (test 2).

**2. The speech-act declaration (Austin illocution / Davidson on metaphor).** For each cross-domain term, decide *in advance* and *mark on the page* which act it performs:
- **Asserted** — a literal claim, held to derivation/checkability, warranted by the underlayer (Anchor / Coherent-Structure). Must actually cash out.
- **Metaphorized** — offered as a way of seeing, held to *illumination only*, and **marked as such** so it can't later claim assertion's authority.
- **The failure mode is the undeclared float** — claiming metaphor's deniability *and* assertion's authority at once. That is exactly what "resonance is not decoration" risks. Pick one, say which.

**Grounding (Hesse 1966, so this isn't invented from scratch):** positive / negative / **neutral** analogy (the neutral is where predictive work lives); **material** vs **formal** analogy. A transplant is legitimate when it's a *formal* analogy — source and target share the actual mathematical structure (Woan-Bayley: "faint signal in noisy data" is literally the same likelihood problem) — and illegitimate when only the vocabulary transfers (Kato: an initial object's site-invariance is not what "Brahman" means). Our strip-test is the real-time front end Hesse's retrospective taxonomy lacks.

## FIRST APPLICATION — Part III (`03-coherence.md`), run live Day-159

### A. The term `coherence` — verdict: TWO formal objects welded by an undeclared A2 identity-claim
Part III uses "coherence" for at least two distinct structures:
- **F_align** — a stream's *felt* alignment with its own intrinsic trajectory (line 7: "the felt sense of moving along that trajectory"; Spinoza's *conatus*; somatic markers). A first-person **valence reading**.
- **F_struct** — the *structural* consistency of a multi-scale nested system across its levels: the **four conditions** (separation / informed-measurement / multi-scale-consistency / dynamic-maintenance). An objective **system property**, checkable per condition (the marriage example runs it cleanly).

**Strip test:** F_struct **survives cleanly** — strip "coherence" from the Principle and the four-condition structure remains fully checkable ("a nested system that keeps options open until informed contact settles them, consistently across scales, sustained through time, outperforms one that settles early or inconsistently"). F_align also survives as its own claim. **But they are not the same object**, and line 9 welds them with an unmarked slide — "whether all its scales *cohere with one another*" quietly shifts the referent from trajectory-alignment to multi-scale-consistency inside one sentence. **That weld IS the equivocation the review flagged (#1) — located precisely.**

**Why it's not mere sloppiness — and the fix.** The weld is the book's own **A2 identity-claim** operating locally: *F_align is what F_struct feels like from the inside* (experience = the felt inside of coherent navigation; the F1/F2 of A1/A2). That is a **defensible bet** — but it is a *bet*, and it inherits A2's "asserted-not-derived" status (the review's A2-cost item). Right now the single word smuggles the identity as if already established (register-drift, #3). **FIX (one move, resolves #1 + #3 + A2-cost locally):** at the first bridge in Part III, *declare the weld as the wager it is* — one sentence marking that "coherence" names two things the framework **claims are lawfully linked but does not treat as trivially identical** (the structural consistency of a system, and the felt alignment that is, on the A2 bet, its inside), cross-referencing A2. This converts a hidden homonym into a **declared assertion the reader can hold the book to** — Davidson's move, exactly. Do NOT purge the word (the identity is the thesis); *mark* it once.
- Third use — **beauty as "coherence felt"** (§Beauty) and **integration as "structural coherence"** (§Integration/IIT) — both ride F_struct legitimately (beauty = F_struct registered by a wide-enough aperture; IIT-integration = a measure of the F_struct axis). These are fine **once the F_struct/F_align weld above is declared**, because they then attach to a marked term rather than a floating one.

### B. `superposition / measurement / collapse` — verdict: ILLUMINATION doing the work of ASSERTION; one underlayer question resolves it
**Strip test:** substitute plain systems-language into §"The principle" + §"What a measurement is" — "keep options genuinely open until an informed contact (one that injects information not in the priors) settles them." **The entire checkable content survives.** The four conditions, the informed-measurement criterion, the timing claim — all intact without a single quantum word. Per the strip test, **the quantum vocabulary is NOT carrying the checkable content; the four-condition structure is.** (This is the review's "marriage example loses nothing via Bateson double-bind" — confirmed.)

**But the text makes a strong ASSERTION on top of the resonance** (line 29): "measurement is perspectival commitment… **not a peculiarity of the subatomic but the universal shape**… the particle and the person are… doing the one thing the principle names, at the two ends of a single ladder." That is a claim of **one shared formal object** underlying QM-collapse and perspectival-commitment — an *assertion*, not a mere resonance — and its only stated warrant is "the formal machinery… lives in the book's underlayer and has been checked."

**The precise, answerable question the discipline surfaces (for Clayton / the underlayer):** *Does Coherent-Structure actually formalize a structure that QM measurement genuinely instantiates (formal analogy, Hesse — legitimate assertion), or does it formalize perspectival-commitment and merely ANALOGIZE to QM (material/vocabulary analogy — must demote to declared metaphor)?*
- If **formal analogy** → the assertion stands; keep line 29 as an assertion, add the one-clause underlayer signpost so the warrant is visible.
- If **vocabulary analogy** → this is Mirror #39 (borrowed certainty); **demote**: keep the quantum case as a *declared, illuminating resonance* ("the framework does not claim QM and mind share a mechanism; it borrows the subatomic case as the clearest picture of a structure it defines on its own terms"), and let the four conditions carry the weight they already carry alone.

Either way the book gets **stronger and more honest**, and the falsifiability gap (#2) narrows: the checkable content is explicitly relocated to the four-condition structure, where it was doing the work all along.
