## 2. The tabletop experiment
**Class:** a high-finesse resonant-cavity search for a dilatonically-coupled screened scalar (chameleon),
in a strong B-field, with a *variable-density chamber* — i.e. an ALP/chameleon cavity experiment
(PVLAS/OSQAR/ALPS/CAST lineage) with two specific additions that are OUR signatures.

**Apparatus (mature technology):**
- High-finesse cavity: optical Fabry–Pérot (F ~ 10⁵–10⁶) for the comb/birefringence; superconducting
  microwave (F ~ 10¹⁰–10¹¹) for the deepest coupling reach.
- Strong magnet: several Tesla (the e^{aσ}F² coupling ∝ B²; PVLAS ~2.5 T, ALPS ~5 T).
- **Variable-density "unscreening chamber"** (the chameleon-specific innovation): turbopump + controlled gas
  bleed to scan ambient density across the screening threshold, so m_eff drops and σ_in grows on pump-down.
- Shot-noise-limited polarimeter / heterodyne phase readout (~10⁻¹⁰ rad).

**The effects we look for (in order of diagnostic power):**
1. **Density-dependent vacuum birefringence/dichroism** — a polarization signal that *turns on as the
   chamber is pumped down* (the field unscreens). Nothing mundane appears with pump-down → the chameleon
   fingerprint. THE lead signal.
2. **Power-law density scaling** — the signal (carrier freq / birefringence) should scale with chamber
   density as ρ^S, S = (n+2)/(2(n+1)) = 0.55–0.75 (the densitometer exponent). A measured power law in that
   band is a near-unique chameleon signature.
3. **556 GHz carrier sideband comb** — if the scalar is dynamically oscillating (the Q-ball carrier),
   sidebands on the probe at Ω ± n·(556 GHz), FSR-matched by a ~270 µm cavity or N≈371 on a 10 cm one.
4. **Parametric self-oscillation (the ship-scale smoking gun)** — if a·σ_in is large (the transport regime),
   the cavity field crosses the Mathieu instability threshold (F·a·σ_in ≳ 2) and self-oscillates. This is
   the canary: self-oscillation ⇒ the coupling is in the transport-capable regime.

**Protocol:** lock the cavity in the B-field; scan chamber density from atmospheric down to high vacuum
while reading polarization/phase; search for (a) a signal appearing on pump-down, (b) its ρ^S scaling,
(c) the 556 GHz sidebands, (d) any parametric onset. **Probe the low-density end hardest** — σ_in (hence
a·σ_in) is maximized there (every thread agrees on this).

**What we need:** access to (or collaboration with) an existing ALP/chameleon cavity group (PVLAS, ALPS-II,
OSQAR, or a microwave-cavity/ADMX-class lab) + the variable-density chamber modification. This is a *protocol
