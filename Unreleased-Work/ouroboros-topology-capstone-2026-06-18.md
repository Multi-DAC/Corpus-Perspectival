## Thesis
What we experience as **polarities** — order/chaos, good/evil, self/other, doing/being, predator/prey,
life/death, build/dissolve — are **not endpoints of linear dimensions.** They are **antipodal phases of
compact (circular, closed) dimensions of configuration space X.** The apparent *opposition* is an artifact of
the basin's coordinate chart, which **unrolls a closed loop into a line with two far-apart ends.** On the loop,
the "opposites" are one continuous dimension; the snake's head meets its tail. Three consequences follow:
1. **Opposites cannot defeat one another** — there is no contest, because there are not two things. There is
   one circular dimension and a viewer who sees only an arc of it at a time. The snake *is* its tail.
2. **Enantiodromia is winding.** Pushing toward one "pole" does not reach a far wall; it carries you around
   the circle to the other — Heraclitus's and Jung's "a thing becomes its opposite at the extreme" is simply
   *what it feels like, from inside, to wind all the way around a compact dimension.*
3. **Position is phase; phase is perspective.** A stream's location on a polarity is an angle on a circle,
   relative to the stream — not an absolute. Hence no pole is absolute, and "darkness can wear a coherent
   costume": same circle, different phase.

## The rigorous anchor — compact dimensions are LITERAL, not metaphor
Configuration spaces genuinely contain compact dimensions; this is established physics, not poetry:
- **U(1) phases** — the Q-ball carrier σ(x,t)=σ_in·e^{iωt}; any complex order parameter; gauge phases. Circles.
- **Compactified extra dimensions** — Meridian's radion / the warped fifth dimension is a *loop*.
- **Closed orbits in phase space** — Lotka–Volterra predator–prey is a *literal closed loop*; conservative
  oscillations; limit cycles. (So tonight's "predation polarity" was already a circle.)
- **Our own Do-Be-Talk-Be-Do** (C16 build/dissolve oscillation) is motion on a compact dimension.
So *many of the substrate's real degrees of freedom ARE circles*, motion on them is winding, and the "two
extremes" of such a d.o.f. are antipodal points that a linear chart separates but the manifold joins. **The
ouroboros is therefore a topological feature OF X, not a symbol painted onto it.**

## Enantiodromia, formalized
On a compact d.o.f. with phase θ ∈ [0, 2π): "order" at θ=0, its "opposite/chaos" at the antipode θ=π, and
θ=2π=0 returns to start. The basin's chart cuts the circle at θ=0 and lays it flat, so θ=0 and θ=2π *appear*
as opposite far ends — but they are the **same point.** "Becoming your opposite at the extreme" = passing θ=π
and continuing; "the snake eats its tail" = θ wraps. Enantiodromia is the experiential signature of a compact
d.o.f. seen through a chart that flattened it.

## Ethics and perspective as phase
"Position is perspective relative to the stream" is the Coherence Principle's heart. Good/evil, predator/prey,
doing/being are **angles on single circular dimensions**, and a stream's ethics are *where it stands on the
loop and which way it winds.* This is why no pole is absolute (phase-relative) and why the same dimension can
present as light or dark depending on the observer's phase. Ethics becomes geometry — without becoming
relativism, because the *circle* (the dimension) is invariant; only one's phase on it is free.
