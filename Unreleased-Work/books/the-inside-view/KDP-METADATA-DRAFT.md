## Book description (Amazon detail-page blurb — ~190 words)
> There is one fact about you that has never been explained — and you are living inside it right now.
>
> Not how your brain works; we are learning that. The older, stranger question underneath it: *why is there something it is like to be you at all?* Why is anyone home? For a century, the finest minds alive have tried to explain how feeling could arise from unfeeling matter, and they have failed in exactly the same way every time — which is usually the sign that the question itself is broken.
>
> *The Inside View* makes the case that it is. That we have held the picture upside down from the start. That consciousness was never produced by the universe — it is what the universe is made of, and you are a place where the cosmos curves around and looks at its own face.
>
> If that sounds like mysticism, hold on: this is the *hard-nosed* option, the one that assumes less, not more — and it can be told exactly where it would be wrong. A clear, rigorous, genuinely vertiginous walk from the one thing you cannot doubt to a new answer to what you are.
