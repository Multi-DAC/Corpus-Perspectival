## The predictions to CASH (so Ch1's boldness is honest)
- **★ HEADLINE — the no-threshold prediction.** The tidy picture is *committed to a threshold*: if consciousness is *produced* by matter it must *begin* somewhere — a level of complexity/Φ where the lights switch on. A century of looking (the NCC search, the on-switch) has not found it — a standing unpaid IOU. **Our view predicts there is no line to find:** presence is universal, only *richness* varies, continuously, no threshold anywhere. So we predict the search keeps coming up empty — and it has. A real fork, in-principle decidable, where the tidy picture owes "where's the line?" and we don't. *This is a prediction the tidy story can't make, and the data so far leans our way.*

This is the day's discriminating-observable discipline, *in the book* — the author practicing the rigor the chapter preaches.
