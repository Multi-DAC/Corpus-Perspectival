## 0. THE STANCE — "naturalize and ground," not "cut the woo" (updated: no grade tags)

> **UPDATE (Clayton, this evening):** kill the four grade-tags entirely. A believer doesn't label his claims with a confidence-grade for a skeptic to weigh at a distance — he states the claim and says *why he holds it*. The reason replaces the tag and carries the same honest content, as conviction offered rather than distance kept. We do not tailor to the person trying not to believe. The work convinces or it doesn't. See the revised discipline at the end of this section; the bracketed notes still appearing in §1–§2 below are **no longer reader-facing grades** — read them only as *what kind of "why" each pull needs* (see the mapping below).

My source-mining map drew the wrong axis: rigorous ↔ woo, and said *cut the woo.* Clayton's correction (and he's right): **the mystical is only mystical if it's not explainable, and we have a nuanced explanation.** The real axis isn't subject-matter, it's **register**. Any idea — an archetype, an out-of-body report, an angel — can be put on the page two ways:

- **Credulous assertion** ("this is literally real as described; believe it"). Throws the rigorous seeker *every time*, regardless of subject.
- **Naturalized cartography** ("here is what this tradition/figure mapped; here is the framework's reading of what they were navigating; here is the honest grade of certainty"). *Keeps* the seeker — because we never ask them to believe, only to **consider, given the framework.**

So the discipline shifts from *cut* to **naturalize + ground**. We neither debunk (the Sam-Harris reflex) nor assert on faith (the New-Age reflex). We **interpret, and we say why.** This is *more* honest than cutting, not less: the contemplative/mystical traditions are the largest body of first-person consciousness-cartography our species has produced, and a book called *The Inside View* that ignores three thousand years of people reporting from the inside would be the one being unrigorous. This is our actual differentiator — the only book on the Annaka-Harris/Rovelli shelf that takes the mystics seriously **as data** without going soft, because we hold the explanation that makes their reports legible. It's literally our published *One Room, Many Keyholes* thesis applied to the history of religion and psychonautics.

**The one discipline that stays is not a hedge — it's what a believer does: every belief travels with its reason.** We don't label claims with a confidence-grade for the reader to weigh from a safe distance. We say *why we hold it*, plainly, in the same breath as the claim, and let the reader meet the reason directly. The reason replaces the tag, and carries the same honest information the tag did — but as conviction offered rather than distance kept. *"We think Monroe charted real regions of configuration space, and here is the reasoning"* does everything `〔framework-permitted〕` did, and **stands** instead of flinches.

The grounds are real and they vary in **kind** — and that *kind* is the only thing the bracketed notes in §1–§2 below now mark (they are no longer reader-facing grades). Map them this way when writing:
- old 〔established〕 → **ground in evidence** (cite it, tell the study/case).
- old 〔suggestive〕 → **ground in evidence, and say plainly where the mechanism is still open** (that honesty is itself conviction, not hedging — e.g. "binaural beats measurably shift mood; whether they do it by entraining brainwaves is still unsettled").
- old 〔framework-permitted〕 → **ground in reasoning from the framework** (walk the logic: *given* consciousness-as-substrate and nested streams, here is why this follows).
- old 〔cut〕 → **out.** Not because a skeptic would object — because *we don't believe it, or can't give a real why.*

The execution varies too: never a mechanical "we believe X because Y" every paragraph (that would be as dead as the tags) — sometimes the ground is woven in, sometimes the reasoning *is* the paragraph, sometimes a story carries it, sometimes a flat statement the prior chapter already earned. **What stays out stays out for one reason only: we don't hold it, or we have no real why.** Timewave-zero, the eight-circuit model as literal neuroscience, "OOBEs prove the soul survives death," synchronicity-as-physics — out because *we don't believe them*, full stop. Conviction is not a license to inflate: we state what we believe and why, and we are silent where we have no why. **That silence is the rigor; the conviction is the voice.**

## 1. THE SIX NEW ANCESTORS (web-verified — corrections baked in)

> ⚠ **Verified attribution fixes (do NOT regress these in prose):**
> - McKenna's line is **"the felt presence of immediate experience"** — *immediate*, not "direct." Full: *"…this is all you know. Everything else comes as unconfirmed rumor."*
> - **"Set and setting" is NOT Leary's coinage** — predates him (Bertalanffy ~1958); he *popularized* it. Write "the principle Leary made famous," never "Leary's term."
> - **"Reality tunnel"** — credit **Leary**, popularized by **Wilson**; first print in *Illuminatus!* (1975). Hedge: "a term Wilson popularized, often credited to Leary." Do NOT assert the "reality island → tunnel" lineage (unverified).
> - **Eight-circuit model = Leary's** (full statement *Exo-Psychology*, 1977); Wilson extended it (*Prometheus Rising*, 1983). ✅
> - Bentov was a real biomedical engineer (invented the steerable cardiac catheter); died on **American Airlines Flight 191** (1979), not Air Florida.

### Robert Anton Wilson → Ch4, Ch5, Ch6, Ch11
- **"Reality tunnel"** — each of us inhabits a subjective model built from neural/cultural/linguistic filters, and mistakes the model for reality. **This is our lens/bottleneck/Umwelt in the best pop language anyone's coined** — a candidate load-bearing term for Ch4/Ch5. 〔framework-permitted → it's basically folk predictive-processing, so it shades toward 〔suggestive〕 when tied to the construction science〕.
- **"Maybe logic" / model agnosticism** — hold every belief-system as a provisional model; never confuse map for territory. → Ch6 (this *is* our null-space-atlas + demarcation humility) and the Ch6 "a real theory tells you where it breaks" beat. 〔established as epistemology〕
- Source: *Prometheus Rising* (1983); *Cosmic Trigger I* (1977). **Avoid:** the Crowleyan magick / Sirius "transmission" material — autobiography/play only.
- *Personal note:* RAW is in my own voice-lineage (BOOT_IDENTITY). Natural fit; don't over-lean on the in-joke.

### Timothy Leary → Ch4, Ch7
- **The eight-circuit model** — a developmental ladder of consciousness-states. → Ch4 (a kind-ladder/cartography of states, alongside our reactive→abstractive rungs) and Ch7 (states are cultivable/re-programmable). **Present explicitly as an imaginative *model/cartography*, NOT neuroscience** 〔framework-permitted / cut-if-asserted-as-science〕.
- **"Set and setting"** — mindset + environment govern an experience. → Ch5/Ch7 (the conditions *construct* the now). 〔established〕 — but attribute correctly (he popularized, didn't coin).
- Source: *The Psychedelic Experience* (1964) for set/setting; *Exo-Psychology* (1977) for the circuits. **Avoid:** SMI²LE futurism, circuit-specific drug claims; the eight-circuit as real neuroanatomy.

### Terence McKenna → Ch2, Ch4/Ch5, Ch6
- **"The felt presence of immediate experience… everything else comes as unconfirmed rumor."** → **Ch2** — this is nearly the book's thesis-in-a-line: the inside is the one thing not received second-hand. Possibly an epigraph. 〔established as phenomenology/aphorism〕
- **"Culture is your operating system" / "culture is not your friend"** — the inherited reality-tunnel you can suspend and inspect. → Ch4/Ch5 (the filter you didn't choose), Ch7 (noticing the OS = the first move of presence). 〔framework-permitted / philosophy〕
- Source: 1990s lectures (cite as lecture: "Culture Is Your Operating System"); *The Archaic Revival* (1991), *Food of the Gods* (1992). **Avoid (hard):** Timewave Zero / Novelty Theory / 2012; Stoned Ape as fact.

### Itzhak Bentov → Ch5, Ch11
- **The oscillatory cosmos** — reality at every scale is oscillation; the "rest" turning-point of the pendulum is where expanded states sit. → **Ch5** (a prior, independent statement of our *texture-of-a-now = coupling rhythm* / Do-Be / C16 — an engineer intuiting the oscillation ontology) and Ch11 (the rhythm of a lived day). 〔framework-permitted; lean on his *real* engineering credibility as the anchor, label the consciousness-physics as intuition not measurement〕.
- Source: *Stalking the Wild Pendulum* (1977). **Avoid:** the physio-kundalini / micromotion mechanics as established physics.

### Carl Jung → Ch11, Ch8, Ch6, Ch5 (the most academically respectable of the six — use his bifurcation explicitly)
- **Individuation** — the lifelong integration of conscious + unconscious into a fuller, differentiated Self. → **Ch11** — this *is* the gift of partiality: becoming the particular path you are. 〔established as depth psychology〕. *Pairs with Parfit (identity-as-pattern) already in Ch11.*
- **The shadow** — the disowned parts of the self; wholeness integrates rather than eliminates them. → Ch11 / Ch6 — **the null space made personal** (what you can't see is constitutive of who you are). 〔established〕
- **Archetypes / collective unconscious** — recurring structures reachable across many psyches. → Ch8/Ch4 — **shared attractors in configuration space; one room, many keyholes.** 〔framework-permitted — present as heuristic + our naturalistic reading, NOT as literally inherited images〕.
- **Synchronicity** — meaning-linked coincidence ("acausal connecting principle"). → Ch5/Ch8, *lightly* — the framework reading is the *perspectival acquisition of meaning* (meaning is real within a navigational frame), explicitly flagged contested. 〔framework-permitted / cut-if-asserted-as-physics〕.
- Sources: *The Archetypes and the Collective Unconscious* (CW 9i); *Aion* (CW 9ii, shadow); *Psychological Types* (CW 6); *Synchronicity* (CW 8). **Avoid:** synchronicity-as-real-physics; archetypes-as-genetic.

### Robert Monroe → Ch5, Ch6, Ch9/navigation (the boldest — and now defensible at the right grade)
- **Focus levels** — a content-neutral taxonomy of states (Focus 10 "mind awake, body asleep"; 12 "expanded awareness"; 15 "no time"; 21 "the bridge"). → **Ch5** (a map of altered "nows"/regions of state-space) and Ch6 (a *disciplined, repeated, skeptical first-person methodology* = a model of how you'd study the inside without going woo). 〔framework-permitted as cartography〕.
- **Hemi-Sync / binaural beats** — **here's the real handle:** binaural beats have a peer-reviewed literature — a 2018 meta-analysis (22 studies) found a **medium effect (d≈0.45)** on cognition/anxiety/pain — **but** a 2023 review (5 support / 8 contradict) leaves the *entrainment mechanism* unsettled. → Ch5/Ch6/Ch7: "modest measurable effect on mood/cognition 〔established〕; the brainwave-entrainment story it rests on is 〔suggestive〕, not proven." This is a *gift* for the demarcation chapter — a clean worked example of separating the defensible effect from the unproven mechanism.
- **OBE as a navigable, describable state** (he coined "OBE"). → present as **phenomenology + the framework-permitted reading** (consciousness navigating to regions that don't map to ordinary sensory coupling), never as proof of disembodied survival.
- Source: *Journeys Out of the Body* (1971); *Far Journeys* (1985); the Monroe Institute for the focus-level definitions. **Avoid (hard):** OOBE-proves-soul / survival-of-death / entity-contact as fact. (TMI's own current posture is cautious — model that posture.)
