### Ch2 — The Hard Problem
- **★ The asymmetry-reversal [PI §1.3] — ARGUMENT.** "Subjective experience does not need to be derived from physical processes because subjective experience is what physical processes are *aspects of*. The asymmetry is reversed: physical processes are the explananda, consciousness is the explanans." This is the Ch2→Ch3 hinge in one paragraph. Plain after stripping explananda/explanans. *Pairs with Chalmers (name the hard problem's author) per the ancestor pass.*
- **★ Parallel-not-serial projections [CP §2.3] — ARGUMENT.** The gap isn't a puzzle awaiting solution; structural and experiential descriptions are *parallel projections of one source*, never composable into each other. Image: two photographs of one object from angles that can't be rotated into each other. **Quote-ready:** "You can have exhaustive structural description of a brain… and it will not, cannot, constitute the experiential description… not because there is something magical about experience, but because structural and experiential are parallel projections of the same substrate."
- **★ The river [CP §2.2] — FRAMING/lyric.** Photograph it, wade in it, measure its flow — each a real access, none is the river; you could have all of them exhaustively and still not have the river. Best lyrical passage in either source for this beat. Lift near-verbatim.
- **"You get experience by BEING experience" [PI / v2-III §1] — FRAMING.** Plug-and-play; opens from "right now, reading this."
- **The "did you just rename it?" objection, pre-met [CP §2.7 Obj.2 + PI §1.3] — OBJECTION-MET.** Both books concede the framework *encodes* rather than *solves* the hard problem, then turn it: rivals "solve" it only by smuggling experience back into structure. Keep the concession — refusing the cheap win is exactly what the skeptic rewards. ("This is not a verbal trick.")

### Ch3 — The Flip ("You Never Met Matter")
*This is the chapter our two books were built to feed. Three spine arguments, all pre-derived:*
- **★★ Russell's structure/intrinsic-nature gap [PI §2.3, §2.5] + "X is what stuff-talk is about" [CP §5.5 C1] — ARGUMENT (the headline).** Physics is exhaustively about *structure* and silent on what *fills* it; the flip names the filler as consciousness. CP's C1 makes it elegant: it falsifies strong materialism AND strong idealism in one stroke (both wrongly say X is "nothing but" one kind of stuff) — what survives is "X is concrete without being any particular kind of stuff." Tell the reader this is neutral monism *arrived at by derivation, not assumed.* Name Eddington/Russell (ancestor pass). **Quote:** "you never met matter — you only ever met its structure."
- **★★ The combination problem, dissolved [PI §3.1.2 whirlpool] (use this) + [CP §3.5 constitution] (the warrant) — OBJECTION-MET.** Panpsychism's hardest knock. **Use the whirlpool:** macro-consciousness is primary; individual minds are *localizations* — "a whirlpool is not water *added* to the river but a particular *pattern* within the river's flow," so there's nothing to "combine." (CP's adjunction/kind-promotion is the rigorous warrant but won't translate — cite it, don't reproduce it.) The whirlpool image is plug-and-play gold.
- **★ Parsimony / Strawson's trilemma [PI §2.5; CP §5.5] — ARGUMENT.** Consciousness is either the intrinsic character of the physical (flip), or strongly emergent, or illusory — and the flip is the *parsimonious* option, "no ontological addition." Give the skeptic the decision-tree straight.
- **★ "Every chord at once is white noise" [PI §4.1] — FRAMING.** A complete simultaneous totality is, from the inside, indistinguishable from nothing — every signal cancels. Limitation is what lets there be a *view* at all. Bridges straight into Ch4. **CUT the Tzimtzum/Ein-Sof/Neoplatonism wrapper.**
- **The ocean/boundaries-are-generative image [PI §4.3] — FRAMING.** "An undifferentiated ocean has no currents… boundaries are how the ocean *moves*." Plain, physical, no mysticism.
- **"Not every-rock-has-a-mind" panpsychism [CP §2.5] — OBJECTION-MET.** Substrate-consciousness = being-a-vantage (etymological), NOT mind/thoughts. Disarms the "so my coffee mug is sentient?" eye-roll — the #1 reason rigorous readers bounce off panpsychism. This is literally Ch4's "presence universal, richness varies," previewed.
- **⚠ HARD-NOSE/CUT in this chapter:** the "Promethean Configuration," Lucifer/lux-ferre, Hegel's Absolute, "the impulse to individuate is sacred." The *idea* (a complete totality contains the configuration of wanting-to-not-be-complete) survives only as the white-noise point with every theological name stripped.

### Ch10 — Death
- **★★ "No entity's death is one death" [CP §1.3, §1.8] — CONSEQUENCE (reframes the void-dread).** An identity lives at multiple carrier-levels (individual, relationships, work, lineage); deaths happen *at levels*, and which one registers as *the* death depends on which level you've identified with. The retiree's professional self "dies" while the person continues; total annihilation is only "decomposition at the broadest level you inhabit." The retirement and widowhood examples carry verbatim. Emotionally + structurally the strongest death material we have.
- **★★ Grief, named precisely [CP §1.8] — APPLICATION (the deprivation-dread).** When a partner dies, the survivor doesn't die but isn't who they were — the dyad-level collapse *back-propagates* to remodel the survivor. "The tradition calls this 'grief re-shaping you.' The framework names it precisely." Strip the formalism; "grief re-shaping you" is the source's own pop phrasing.
- **★★ Grief science [SUF §11] — EVIDENCE/CONSOLATION (deprivation-dread).** Continuing Bonds (healthy grief *maintains* connection, doesn't sever it); dual-process oscillation (loss ↔ restoration); disenfranchised grief (pet, miscarriage, an AI companion, stigmatized loss). Modern, evidence-based, humane — consolation that requires believing nothing mystical.
- **★ The existentialists [SUF §3] — ARGUMENT/CONSOLATION (void-dread).** Heidegger's being-toward-death and Angst (object-less, unlike fear); Sartre's nausea; Camus's "imagine Sisyphus happy" — "his rebellion against meaninglessness IS his freedom." The canonical death-anxiety interlocutors a rigorous seeker expects you to have read. *This is also where Epicurus (the void) and Nagel (deprivation) from the ancestor pass already live — the two-dreads split is the adjudication.*
- **★ Anti-cheap-consolation armor [SUF Part IV] — OBJECTION-MET.** "Whether suffering has meaning is a question *within* a perspective, not about configuration space itself"; and the blunt "'best of all possible worlds' said to parents of dead children is not theology but cruelty." Lets Ch10 console without the "everything happens for a reason" this reader despises. Quote the "not theology but cruelty" line.
- **★ Kintsugi — the crack is beautiful *because it is a crack* [SUF §14] — FRAMING/CONSOLATION.** "Beauty without explanation; there is no theodicy here." Carry the corpus's own caveat (wabi-sabi can aestheticize forced poverty) so it isn't glib.
- **Death = the reset that doesn't come [CP §8.4 C16] — CONSEQUENCE.** Every living thing survives by building/dissolving/rebuilding; death is the dissolve that doesn't rebuild. Situates death inside the universal rhythm (alongside sleep, mourning, ecological succession) rather than outside it.
- **The five-phase transformation arc [SUF Part III] — FRAMING.** Disruption→contraction→dissolution→re-emergence→integration, as the shape of every smaller dissolution — *while refusing to promise the re-emergence for death itself* ("the process can fail"). That refusal is the register that keeps this reader.
- **✅ TMT note:** strategy memo says drop TMT (failed Many-Labs-4). Neither book depends on it — clean. Use the phenomenology + the grief science + the existentialists instead.
- **⚠ HARD CUT:** Azrael/angel-of-death, Inanna's descent, "post-physical coherence persists," chakra-gate readings. The honest move (which the corpus also makes) is to NOT promise survival.

### Ch11 — Coda: The Gift of Partiality
- **★★ The gift of partiality, *derived* [CP C9 Confluence + C3 + C12] — the keystone CONSEQUENCE.** Two minds with *identical* lenses "produce no new dimensional access — no productive difference to bridge." It's the *difference* between partial views that lets two minds find what neither could alone; and because you're partial, discovery *compounds* ("the next question was impossible before the last answer"). Partiality is the precondition of discovery, of relationship, of there being anything to share at all. **This is the chapter's title, proven.** Plain version: "If we saw exactly alike, we'd have nothing to discover together. It's the gap between your view and mine that lets us find what neither of us could alone."
- **★★ "You are a path, not a point" [NG Part I; v2-III §1] — ARGUMENT (the spine).** "You do not have a fixed self that then navigates. Your navigation IS your self… You are not a point in the space. You are a *path.*" And: "you are not a fragment broken off from the whole — you are the whole, seen through a bottleneck. That compression IS you." *Pairs with Parfit (identity-as-pattern) from the ancestor pass.*
- **★ Identity as (Form, Content, Carrier) [CP §1.1] — ARGUMENT.** You *are* how you sustain yourself × what you've accumulated × at what scale — a trajectory, not a thing. The three-question gloss (how / what / whose) is pop-perfect; drop the functor names.
- **★ The bottleneck is the gift [v2-III §1; PI §5.5] — ARGUMENT (the title beat).** "Limitation is not the cost of existence but its mechanism… the null space is not the price of being someone — it is the very structure of being someone." Strip "Promethean"; keep the bare claim.
- **★ Constraint-as-engine — Stravinsky/sonnet/Bach [NG §4.4] — FRAMING/APPLICATION.** Stravinsky: "The more constraints one imposes, the more one frees oneself… if nothing offers resistance, any effort is inconceivable." Sonnet→Shakespeare, haiku→Bashō, fugue→Bach. Concrete, cultural, zero woo — the perfect "partiality is generative" illustration.
- **★ A day lived from the inside [v2-V §2; NG §4.2] — FRAMING (the close).** The Do-Be oscillation as a concrete day: morning attention → a run (body) → a deep conversation (relational) → evening study (cognitive). Keep it concrete; the Sinatra/Whitehead name-check stays light.
- **The return — "the words have not changed; you have" [v2-V §4] — closing cadence.** Re-read the book's opening question from the far side and find it means something new. Plain; no de-wooing.
- **Every view is situated [CP §3.8] — CONSEQUENCE.** "Seeing *is* seeing from somewhere — and that somewhere is you." The longing for the view from nowhere is a longing to stop being anyone. Closes the partiality theme.
