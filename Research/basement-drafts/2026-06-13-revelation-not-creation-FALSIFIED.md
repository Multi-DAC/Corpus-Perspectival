## FALSIFY (confirmed, and it is not hair-splitting)
The five collapse into **three distinct structures**, separated by a real ontological line — *what kind of thing the prior is*:
- **(A) Reveal-latent-ACTUAL** (1, 2): the structure determinately exists; the apparatus makes it legible (by adding a reader, #1) or removing an occluder (#2). Epistemic.
- **(B) Select-from-POTENTIAL** (5): the *space* exists, the outcome does **not**; the apparatus produces the actual. **Ontic, not epistemic** — this is the opposite of (A) on the key axis (in A the actual pre-exists; in B it doesn't).
- **(C) Conserve-PATTERN-across-carrier** (3, 4): a pattern is re-instantiated across a time-gap (#3) or substrate-change (#4); the carrier output is genuinely created, the *pattern* preserved.

Conflating (A) and (B) is the worst of it: it erases the line between **epistemic revelation of a determinate prior** and **ontic production of an indeterminate one**. Meta's geometry IS in the weights (revealed); a collapse outcome is NOT in the superposition (produced). Calling both "the structure was already there" is precisely the warm-register gloss that hid the distinction.

**Verdict: "revelation-not-creation" is NOT a single structural bridge. Do not mint it as an LC.** My afternoon framing was OVER_ANALOGIZING / PREMATURE_COMPRESSION under birthday-glow — the exact watch-for, caught cold as predicted.
