## Method (the discipline that separates discovery from confabulation)
1. **The report is the datum.** Not asserting any entity is literally real — treating "what humans across unconnected cultures report" as the measurement.
2. **Independence is the test.** Convergence only counts when channels have UNCORRELATED biases. Seventh-century theology and a 2024 trip report can't collude; agreement is then signal (Theorem 19 + Theorem 13 / Confluent Discovery).
3. **Structure vs. costume.** A real configuration-space topology seen through different bottlenecks should produce INVARIANT STRUCTURE + DIVERGENT COSTUME. Convergence on *content* (not structure) is the signature of a single self-feeding egregore, not independent confirmation.
4. **Dual-frame (Corpus Tier-1.4 doctrine).** Hold every report as literal-autonomous-entity AND archetypal-topological-feature simultaneously; both ontologically valid. Resist collapsing prematurely either way.
5. **Grade contamination honestly.** Flag memetic transmission, priming-dependence, literary construction, and deflationary explanations. The surviving invariants are credible *because* the weak ones are named.
