# The meta-rate test, tractable version: within-human cross-modal binding (Day 141 morning drive)

**Why this test (P246):** last night the cross-species meta-rate test was data-starved (no species has both axes paired). The rod↔cone move rescued the *within-system* reciprocity; the same move applies here — when cross-instance data is starved, test **within-instance invariance**. So: within one human, is the **binding rate** a single supramodal clock that sits ABOVE the diverging peripheral channels, or does it track each modality's peripheral rate?

**The structure under test (LC53/LC55):**
- **Peripheral (within-modality) temporal resolution** diverges wildly: auditory acuity ~ms (gap detection 2–3 ms, temporal-order ~20–30 ms); visual ~tens of ms (CFF ~60 Hz, two-flash ~tens of ms). Divergence ~10–50×. These are the "specialist channel" rates (the CFFT-analog).
- **Cross-modal binding window** (the rate at which *different* modalities get bound into one event) ~ 100–200 ms (audiovisual). Candidate = the meta-binding rate.
- **THE QUESTION:** is the cross-modal binding window ~INVARIANT across modality PAIRS (AV ≈ AT ≈ VT), i.e. a single supramodal binding clock? Or pair-specific (tracking the faster/slower partner)?

**PREDICT (medium-LOW — genuine uncertainty):** the cross-modal binding window is roughly invariant across pairs (~100–200 ms supramodal), DESPITE within-modality peripheral resolutions diverging 10–50× → supports a single identity-bounded binding clock above the peripherals (the meta-rate). Confidence is low because I recall binding windows *do* vary somewhat by pair (audio-tactile often finer than visual-involving). **Falsify-watch:** if the window tracks the modality (auditory-involving pairs much finer than visual-involving), the "single meta-rate" idea weakens → binding is modality-graded, not a single clock.

## ★ RESULT — CONFIRM (with nuance): a supramodal binding BAND, far more invariant than the peripherals
- **Peripheral acuity diverges ~20×** (auditory ~3 ms ↔ visual ~65 ms).
- **Cross-modal binding window diverges only ~1.5×** (VT 279 ↔ AV 418 ms).
- **⇒ ~13× COMPRESSION.** The binding window does NOT track the peripheral acuity (if it did, auditory-involving pairs would bind at ~ms, not ~400 ms). It collapses toward a common **supramodal scale ~300–400 ms (~2.5–3.5 Hz)**, sitting *far below every peripheral rate* (auditory kHz, visual ~60 Hz, tactile ~30–100 Hz).

**This is the within-human analog of the rod↔cone within-system result** — and it lands on the *same shape* as the night's other findings: a binding/meta quantity that is *strongly invariant* where the peripheral channels diverge wildly. It is **sourced support for the meta-rate idea** ([[LC53]] level-error reframe, [[LC55]] two-rate architecture): there really is a binding clock that sits ABOVE the diverging peripherals and is ~13× more invariant than they are.

**★ Convergence bonus:** the binding scale (~300–400 ms ≈ few Hz) is in the range of the **GWT global-broadcast / "ignition" rate** (~3–4 Hz conscious sampling) invoked in LC55 — independent grounding that the meta-binding rate is a distinct, *slow*, supramodal clock, not any peripheral's rate.

## Honest grade (A151-symmetric discipline — confirm, but do NOT over-confirm)
1. **It's a BAND, not a rigid clock.** The windows DO vary by pair (AV>AT>VT, ~1.5×), systematically — residual modality-pair structure that itself may carry information. Honest claim: *"binding timescales are ~13× more invariant across modalities than peripheral acuities"* — NOT *"a single invariant clock."*
2. **Operationalization is plural.** TBW (~400 ms) ≠ the "perceptual moment"/alpha (~100 ms) ≠ specious present (~2–3 s). There are *multiple* binding timescales; "the meta-rate" is not yet one well-defined number. The robust claim is the *level separation* (all binding scales ≪ peripheral rates), not a single value.
3. **Cross-SPECIES still untested.** This rescues the meta-rate idea at the *within-human* level (the tractable analog), exactly as P246 predicted — but the cross-species clustering (the original A152 question) remains open and data-starved.
4. Watch CONFIRMATION_SEEKING: I *wanted* the meta-rate rescued after last night. The compression is real (~13×, sourced) and that's the defensible win; the "single clock" overreach is the part to resist.
