## The headline: our portal FLOOR mechanism is peer-reviewed — as both ally and null hypothesis

The single most important finding is that **the geophysical floor of our place-threshold mechanism
(rift + subvertical fault + piezo-active rock → EM emission → anomalous light + radon) is established,
instrumented, peer-reviewed science** — and it is *both* the strongest grounding for our survey *and* the
mundane null our portal claim has to beat.

### 1. Earthquake lights are real, and they live exactly where we pinned ⭐ (strongest, mainstream)
**Thériault, St-Laurent, Freund & Derr (2014), *Seismological Research Letters* 85(1):159–178.**
65 best-documented earthquake-light (EQL) cases since 1600 CE: **~85% on or near rifts; ~97% adjacent to
subvertical faults.** Intraplate faults are ~5% of global seismicity but ~97% of documented EQL. This is a
mainstream geophysics venue (GeoScienceWorld/SSA), covered straight by *Nature* news.

This is **our Sandia scoring target, independently confirmed by the literature**: rift environment +
subvertical active fault is precisely the EQL-prone configuration, and it's precisely what the Hubbell
Spring / Sandia / Rincon pins sit on. Our survey wasn't reaching for the exotic to find "where lights
happen" — it reproduced, from gravity + fault data, the *same* selection the EQL catalog made from eyewitness
reports. That is a genuine convergence and it raises the prior that *something luminous* occurs at those pins.

### 2. The mechanism is solid-state physics, and its signature list is our instrument list ⭐
**Freund's peroxy-defect / positive-hole theory** (multiple peer-reviewed: *EPJ Special Topics* 230 (2021);
*Acta Geophysica* 2009; *J. Asian Earth Sci.* / Tectonophysics; arXiv 1502.02300, 1711.01780).
Stressed igneous rock activates peroxy defects → mobile **positive holes** (defect electrons in the O²⁻
sublattice) flow out of the stressed volume → produce **ground potentials, stimulated IR, massive air
ionization, radon emanation, ozone, corona discharge, low-frequency EM emission.**

That is, almost line-for-line, the instrument package I specified for the Sandia visit (magnetometer +
EM-spectrum logger + radon detector). The mechanism that would make our pins "light up" has a named,
quantitative, peer-reviewed solid-state basis. We don't need to invent the EM-generation step; Freund did it.

### 3. Hessdalen hands us the discriminator — recurring light *without* the earthquakes ⭐⭐
**Teodorani (2004), *J. Scientific Exploration* 18:217; Italian Committee for Project Hessdalen (Embla
reports); and a 2024 mainstream VLF-EM survey** (*J. Applied Geophysics*, ScienceDirect S0926985124001149).
Hessdalen is the one place with a permanent instrumented station (since 1984): the lights are **real,
plasma-confirmed by radar + spectrometer + magnetometer**, recurring, place-fixed, seasonal.

The crucial fact: **Hessdalen has essentially no seismicity** (no M>2.5 since 2000), so the pure
EQL/peroxy-stress explanation *fails there* — and researchers say so explicitly. The lights recur at a fixed
place **without** the earthquakes that EQL theory requires; the proposed driver is standing
**piezoelectric + EM** fields, not transient seismic stress.

This is the most valuable single result for us, because it isolates the **discriminating measurement**:
- EQL/peroxy theory predicts light **tracks seismic stress** (transient, quake-correlated).
- Our place-threshold mechanism predicts a **standing** signature at a **density extremum** (ω_pin localizes
  at an extremum, *not* a gradient — `portal-omega-pin-RESULTS-2026-06-17`), persistent and place-fixed
  **independent of seismic stress.**

Hessdalen is a real-world instance where those two predictions **diverge**, and the data lean toward the
standing/place-fixed (non-seismic) reading. That is exactly the residual our mechanism is for. It also
sharpens the Sandia experiment: the test is **not** "is there EM/light at the fault" (EQL already predicts
that — it'd be a confound, not a confirmation) but "is there a **standing** signature pinned to the
density extremum, persisting between seismic events, carrying the **carrier-blueshift sideband** our scalar
predicts and peroxy-holes do not." The discriminator is the *spectral* and *persistence* signature, not the
mere presence of lights.

## The consciousness-correlation leg: high-venue but genuinely unsettled (weakest leg, held open)

### 4. Cardeña (2018), *American Psychologist* 73(5):663–677 — and its honest counter-literature
The APA flagship published a review concluding the psi evidence (ganzfeld, presentiment, RNG) is "comparable
to that of accepted phenomena" and not explained away by quality/fraud/selective-reporting. High venue, real.
**But** the counter-literature is strong and I weight it: no independent replication consensus on ganzfeld;
the Bem (2011) precognition studies are a *founding case* of the psychology replication crisis; documented
decline effect, selective reporting, and "psi effects correlate with poor study quality" (the damning one).
A 2025 Edinburgh methodology thesis and the "Why Most Research Findings About Psi Are False" (PMC7530246)
review lay this out.
**Net grade: contested, not established.** The consciousness-primary / consciousness-coupling invariant —
the spine of the comparative map and of our own framework — does **not** have settled experimental support
from parapsychology. It has a serious-venue review pointing one way and a serious replication literature
pointing the other. This is the framework's **weakest empirical leg** and it stays explicitly open. I will
not lean on psi as if it were measured.

## The instrumented-UAP leg: rigorous, and honestly NULL so far

### 5. The Galileo Project (Loeb, Harvard) — the gold-standard method, no anomaly yet
First observatory commissioning data (Jan–May 2024): **~500,000 aerial objects**, all resolved to mundane
classes (birds, balloons, drones, aircraft, satellites) by the ML pipeline; *Journal of Astronomical
Instrumentation* overview + arXiv 2502.06794. This is the right way to do it — open hardware, open data,
peer review — and the honest current state of rigorous instrumented UAP work is **null**: nothing anomalous
established. I weight this *as data*. It is the proper antidote to the testimony batch: when you actually
instrument the sky carefully, so far you get birds.
