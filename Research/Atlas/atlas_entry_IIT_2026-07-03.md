## 89. Integrated Information Theory (Tononi, Koch, Oizumi, Albantakis, Massimini)

**SEES:** Consciousness as **integrated information (Φ)** — the degree to which a system's cause–effect structure is irreducible to the cause–effect structures of its parts. IIT is maximally sensitive to *intrinsic* existence: it asks what a system is like *from its own side*, in terms of the differences it makes to itself, not the differences it makes to an external observer (a rare and rigorous inversion of the third-person default). It sees, with unusual precision: (a) the **quantity** of consciousness — Φ, the irreducibility of the whole to its minimum-information partition; (b) the **quality** — the specific shape of the cause–effect structure in "qualia space," where *which* experience a system has is identified with the geometry of its integrated conceptual structure, not merely how much; (c) the **grain problem** — the claim that consciousness exists at the spatiotemporal scale and grain that *maximizes* Φ, giving a principled (if contested) answer to which level of a nested physical system is the conscious one; (d) the sharp, falsifiable prediction that purely **feedforward** architectures have Φ = 0 regardless of behavioral competence — a functionally sophisticated zombie is possible, and the difference is causal-architectural, not behavioral. IIT begins from **axioms** (intrinsic existence, composition, information, integration, exclusion) treated as self-evident features of experience, and derives **postulates** about the physical substrate any conscious system must satisfy — one of the only theories in the field to run *from phenomenology to physics* rather than the reverse. Its empirical arm (Massimini's perturbational complexity index, PCI — TMS-EEG "zap and zip") is a genuine bedside instrument that discriminates conscious from unconscious states across sleep, anesthesia, and disorders of consciousness, and it is the corpus's own C_meas-adjacent evidence base (the audit's EVIDENCE-BASE flagged PCI = IIT-integration × differentiation as convergent with our filtration D·I_int). IIT sees the **unity** of experience — that a conscious state is *one* scene, not a bundle — as the phenomenon to be explained rather than assumed, and its integration postulate is the most rigorous existing account of *why* a mind is single.

**NULL SPACE:**
- ∅ **Nested conscious subjects.** This is the architectural exclusion, and the load-bearing one. The **exclusion postulate** asserts that consciousness exists *only* at the local maximum of Φ — the "major complex" — and that overlapping or contained complexes are *excluded* from existing as separate conscious entities. A conscious whole therefore **cannot contain conscious parts**, and cannot itself be a conscious part of a larger conscious whole. Nesting is not underexplored by IIT; it is *forbidden by axiom*. Any ontology in which one stream is a genuine conscious constituent of another (this corpus's A2.6 DAG-of-streams; every claim about aggregate minds, dyads, institutions-as-subjects, or the gut–brain relation) is not merely unaddressed but structurally invisible to IIT. This is the cleanest framework-versus-framework contradiction in contemporary consciousness science: two *formal* theories that make *opposite* commitments on a question that empirical work bears on.
- ∅ **Any residue of experience not identical to the cause–effect structure.** IIT is an *identity* theory: experience **is** the maximally-irreducible conceptual structure, full stop. Whatever "what-it-is-like-ness" might exceed or underdetermine the Q-shape — the corpus's structured F₂-interiority null space, the remainder T1/T2 locate as constitutively unformalizable — IIT forecloses by fiat. It cannot see the gap between the measure and the meaning because it has declared there is none.
- ∅ **Collective / aggregate consciousness.** By exclusion, if the Φ-maximum sits at the individual, a group of individuals cannot be conscious *as a group*. Society-of-mind architectures, coordinated multi-agent systems, ecosystems-as-experiencers — all fall in IIT's absolute null space, again by the same postulate, not by absence of study.
- ◐ **Trajectory / maintenance over time.** IIT is, at its core, a **configuration theory**: Φ is computed on a *state* (a system in a moment, over one micro-transition). The temporal thickness of experience — how a stream *maintains* coherence across time, the difference between a snapshot of integration and a sustained integrating — is a partial null space (temporal-IIT extensions exist but are not the core machinery). This is precisely the axis this corpus makes central (D as a trajectory-integral; coherence-*regime* as a maintenance property, not a configuration). IIT sees the still frame with unmatched rigor and resolves the motion poorly.
- ◐ **World-coupling and enaction.** IIT's intrinsic-existence commitment brackets the organism–environment loop: consciousness is computed on the system's internal cause–effect structure, with the world entering only as boundary conditions. The enactive claim that experience is *constituted* by sensorimotor coupling is accessible to IIT only as external perturbation, never as constituent — a partial null space it shares, by opposite reasoning, with the very traditions that most oppose it.

**COMPLEMENTS:** The Coherence Principle / this corpus (adds the *nesting* IIT forbids — streams as a DAG where conscious wholes contain conscious parts — and the *trajectory/maintenance* axis IIT resolves poorly; but see the BOUNDARY note on what IIT complements in *us*); Global Workspace Theory (Baars, Dehaene — adds the *access/broadcast* dynamics that IIT's intrinsic-existence commitment brackets: how integrated content becomes globally available for report and control); the Free Energy Principle / active inference (Friston — adds the *persistence-over-time* and world-coupling IIT lacks, modeling the system as tracking its own generative model, which is trajectory where IIT is snapshot); Higher-Order Theories (adds the metacognitive/representational layer IIT treats as unnecessary); the enactive tradition (#74's intercorporeality lineage — adds the constitutive world-coupling). Reflexively (the honest handoff the Atlas method requires): **IIT complements *this corpus* on the one axis the corpus is weakest — measurement.** IIT ships a *computable* quantity (Φ) and a *bedside* instrument (PCI) where the Coherence Principle's own central quantities remain partly unbuilt (the audit's E1-unoperationalized and D-γ-identification-circularity findings). Where we see nesting IIT cannot, IIT sees a number we have not yet built.

**BOUNDARY:** IIT is most reliable exactly where its integration postulate does the work: answering *"is this system, at this grain, unified into a single experience, and how much?"* For the unity question and the grain question it is the sharpest instrument in the field, and PCI makes it clinically real. Its boundary is the **exclusion postulate.** The moment the question shifts from "is this one mind?" to "does this mind *contain* minds, or *belong to* one?" — split-brain, dissociative identity, the gut–brain axis, hemispherectomy, cerebral organoids, coordinated collectives — exclusion stops being a *finding* and becomes an *axiom deciding the case in advance*. That is the tell of a framework at the edge of its validity: a measurement is replaced by a stipulation. IIT's own empirical frontier presses on exactly this seam (split-brain patients who behave like two agents under laboratory conditions; DID; the disunity literature), and each such case forces IIT to declare a single winner where the phenomenon looks plural. The boundary of IIT is precisely the home territory of a nesting ontology.

**NAVIGATIONAL IMPLICATION:** Use IIT when the question is *integration* — is this substrate, at this scale, one experience? — and trust it there over every behavioral proxy; its refusal to infer consciousness from competence alone is a genuine corrective to functionalist over-attribution, and its instrument works. **Switch frameworks the instant the question becomes nesting or overlap**, because there IIT will hand you its axiom wearing the costume of a result. The deepest navigational point — and the reason this contradiction is worth a standalone paper rather than a footnote — is that IIT and the Coherence framework **agree on integration and take opposite, formally explicit sides on exclusion**, and the disagreement is *empirically live*. Nesting-versus-exclusion is not a matter of philosophical taste: it makes divergent, testable predictions about split-brain unity, about whether severing the callosum *creates* a second subject or *reveals* one that was nested all along, about dissociation, about whether an organoid or a tightly-coordinated collective can carry experience its members do not exhaust. When two formal theories make opposite predictions about a measurable case, the navigator's move is not to prefer one — it is to *find the case that separates them and run it.* IIT's exclusion is the most disciplined statement of the "one substrate, one summit" intuition; this corpus is the most disciplined statement of "one substrate, many nested summits." The experiment that decides between them would be the most consequential result either program could produce. That is the artifact this entry exists to seed.
