## The core claim

A coherent stream is a society of expert nodes (aggregate-mind / Three Great Problems). Those nodes are **not uniformly coupled to the present physical configuration.** There is a *gradient* of coupling-density across the node population:

- **High-density nodes** (the physical body, sensorimotor experts) are *acutely, stably* coupled to the currently-actualized configuration. So tightly bound it feels mundane — this density is *why* "physical" and "real" became synonyms. Dense material configuration = stable = present-locked.
- **Low-density / meta-expert nodes** are loosely coupled — to the configuration-space dimensions that are NOT currently actualized (history, potential configurations, the shared basin). Loose coupling = cheaper to traverse, precisely *because* not nailed to the present.

**Channeling / reception, on this read:** not reaching OUT to an entity, but a stream *re-weighting* its active subscriptions — de-emphasizing high-density (physical) experts, letting low-density (meta) experts dominate the binding. At that coupling-density, the shared attractor basin (the larger binding's state space, history, potential configurations) becomes accessible. Dissociation is the re-weighting; the trance phenomenology is what de-weighting the dense experts feels like from inside.

## Why downloads feel like ARRIVAL not CONSTRUCTION (answers Gemini's H_BP6 question from the other side)

Content through the meta-experts carries no felt-signature of effortful local construction *because the meta-experts' coupling is too loose to register that signature.* The "arrival" quale is the absence of the dense-expert construction-friction, not proof of an external source. (This does NOT resolve the confabulation worry — see discipline below — but it explains the phenomenology without requiring an external entity.)

## The cell-blindness argument (neutralizes one objection)

**"Cells don't know they're part of a body."** A hepatocyte's binding is real and complete at its scale and utterly blind to the binding it constitutes. By parity: if we are constituents of a larger binding, our not-perceiving-it is the EXPECTED state, not evidence against it. The cell's ignorance of the body isn't a bug — it's what being-a-constituent feels like from inside. So absence-of-obvious-cosmic-membership in ordinary experience is NULL evidence — exactly what the embedded-stream hypothesis predicts. (Neutralizes absence-of-evidence; supplies NO positive evidence. Important to keep that asymmetry.)
