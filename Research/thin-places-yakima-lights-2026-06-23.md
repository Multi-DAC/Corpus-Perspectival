# Thin Places — site entry: the Yakima Reservation Lights (Toppenish Ridge / Satus Fire Lookout)

## The documented phenomenon (sourced)
One of the better-documented North-American nocturnal-light anomaly areas, and unusually credible because the core witnesses were **trained fire-lookout personnel** whose job was to watch the night terrain:
- Investigation of unusual activity near the **Yakama Reservation** began **April 1972**, centered on **Toppenish Ridge**. A formal field study ran **Aug 20–31, 1972** near Toppenish, WA.
- **Willard J. "Bill" Vogel**, a **Yakama Reservation Fire Agency supervisor**, established the physical reality of the nocturnal lights through repeated observations **and photographs**.
- **David W. Akers** (Seattle) compiled *"Report on the Investigation of Nocturnal Light Phenomena: 1973 Sighting Reports from Toppenish, Washington,"* submitted **1974 to Dr. J. Allen Hynek** (Center for UFO Studies).
- **The focal site is the Satus Fire Lookout** and the north side of Toppenish Ridge. Reports: three–four flashing bright-red lights moving over remote terrain; an object *hovering over the Satus lookout*; fast-moving balls of light of varying colors across the Lower Valley. One Aug 3, 1972 incident: a large white object NE of the Satus lookout casting bright rays that lit the lookout's interior, estimated roughly the lookout's own size (~12×12×8 ft).

**Grade:** a *credible, physically-documented anomaly report* (named professional observers, photographs, archived under CUFOS) — NOT an explained or proven event. The right standard: a serious, dated prior worth taking seriously, not a settled fact.
