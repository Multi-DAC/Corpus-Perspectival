**Review policy (Clayton, Day 129, governs all remedies):** the published record is
explicitly HISTORICAL — raw process-record, "basis for further work, exploration, and
improvement" (PhilArchive description's own words). Not academic, not peer-review-bound;
honesty is maintained through the open process-track, not retroactive correction. Therefore
**NO errata/versioning of published artifacts.** All remedies apply to CURRENT and FUTURE
work only: translation tables and supersession notes live in current volumes as reader aids;
formal fixes land in the living sources; the historical strata stay as laid down.

- **[FORMAL]** — error in formal content → fix in the LIVING source (repo volumes); published
  historical record stays as-is

- **[PRESENTATION]** — framing, site, versioning clarity → free fixes
- **[PROPAGATES]** — defect inherited by in-progress volumes → tag the downstream volume
- **[LANDS]** — strengths worth protecting in revision (do not lose these fixing the rest)
