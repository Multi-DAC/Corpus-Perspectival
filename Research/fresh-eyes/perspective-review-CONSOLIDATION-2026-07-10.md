## 2. THE META-FINDING (organizes everything below)

**Aesthetic closure is substituting for argumentative closure.** The book's greatest strength — the prose — is the vector of its deepest weakness.

- Gemini-Critic (whole): *"it is so terrified of being untidy… that it occasionally uses its breathtaking prose style to paper over the jagged, non-Euclidean edges of absolute human and computational suffering."*
- Editorial (whole): *"rigor's vocabulary without rigor's receipts"* — *"the prose is the book's glory and its anesthetic."*

Its four masks, each an instance: `do-be-do-be-do` as argumentative escape hatch (§T1-6), the frictionless dual-authored voice (§T1-4), evil-as-geometric-pity (§T1-3), the plenum papering over malheur (§T1-5).

**Direct consequence for the "expand, don't cut" strategy (Clayton's call — endorsed):** the expansion must be **expository and honest-about-the-unsolved, not more lyrical.** Examples that expose the machinery — yes. Rhythm that resolves tension emotionally — no. More beautiful prose *worsens* the papering-over. This is the hard guardrail on the revision.
