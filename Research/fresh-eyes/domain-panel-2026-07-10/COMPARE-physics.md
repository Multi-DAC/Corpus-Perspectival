## The clashes

### 1. The rename test
- **Critic:** rename *superposition → held-open options, measurement → informative contact, collapse → settling* and the Coherence Principle "survives as a **banality**" ("keep options open, then decide well and consistently") — the signature of illicit vocabulary; the quantum words carried **connotative prestige, not propositional content.**
- **Apologist:** the rename **passes decisively** *because the inferential engine is classical sequential analysis (Wald / value-of-information), not quantum formalism (von Neumann)* — "an argument that borrows a word's prestige *breaks* when renamed; this one does not, because its work is carried by a different, fully classical formalism that happens to share the *word*."
- **Verdict — CONVERGE → rename the terms.** These look opposite and are the same finding: **both agree the engine is classical and the quantum words are detachable.** Critic: rename because the vocabulary is illicit. Apologist: rename is *costless* because the structure survives. Same action, and the metaphysics-apologist independently calls it "the single highest-leverage revision." *Fix (near-unanimous across four reviewers): restate the Coherence Principle in decision-theoretic terms it owns; keep the Wald/optimal-stopping structure; drop "superposition/collapse."*

### 2. The QM category error / the particle-person identity
- **Critic:** the **clearest hard error in the book.** "Superposition" is used for a *classical Bayesian posterior* over definite-but-unknown options — structurally the **opposite** of quantum superposition (no interference, no amplitudes, no non-commutativity). Decoherence — the actual physics of superposition→outcome — is *absent*, and on the mainstream reading perspective has **no** privileged role in collapse, so "the most-studied instance" doesn't work the way the general shape requires. This silently underwrites the unearned "particle and person, two ends of one ladder."
- **Apologist (best defense):** the book **already conceded the disanalogy** and narrowed its claim to a shared *abstract shape* (possibility → informing-interaction → actuality-for-an-index), offered explicitly as *a posit, not a proof*; a posit flagged as a posit "cannot be convicted of stealing authority," and the *Note*'s refusal (nearly borrowing quantum authority, pulled back) proves the disclaimers cost the book something.
- **Verdict — CONTESTED → demote the identity to flagged analogy (the apologist's own "schematic-only").** The apologist is right the *shape*-posit is licit *if* nothing downstream depends on the quantum case (the rename test passes). But the critic is right that "two ends of one ladder" **re-loads the loan in the next breath** — it asserts an *identity*, not a flagged analogy. The apologist's own fix ("state the shared skeleton formally *or declare it schematic-only*") is the resolution. *Fix (physics-critic + physics-apologist + metaphysics-apologist agree): cut/demote the particle-person identity to "a suggestive analogy we do not press"; if kept at all, address the interference disanalogy head-on (neither reviewer thinks it can be answered).*

### 3. Optimal-stopping falsifiability — banked, or tautology relocated?
- **Critic:** the reformulation **relocated the tautology from "coherence" into "optimal"** — "systems that collapse at the reward-maximizing time outperform" is analytic by argmax. Genuinely empirical only in the **cancer-cell / multi-scale-consistency** clause (which needs *none* of the quantum words). **New formal defect:** the deceptive-channel example runs *two* incompatible senses of "coherence" — "faithfully integrates" (needed for the inversion story) vs. "stops at t\*" (needed for the falsifiability story) — and they **diverge exactly at that example** (in a deceptive channel t\*=0, so faithful integration *is* premature).
- **Apologist (best defense):** the falsifiability is **banked, not promised** — three contaminated terms externalized onto environment-supplied quantities; the deceptive-channel **inversion** is a *directional* prediction no tautology can make (the "garbling-helps-under-misspecification" phenomenon, Berk's theorem); the cancer cell is a clean multilevel-selection failure; and the whole thing is an instance of the **already-validated marginal-value-theorem / ideal-observer paradigm** (Charnov 1976 — an optimal-stopping law tested for 50 years against organisms that can't compute it). "Promissory note understates it."
- **Verdict — CONTESTED → the kernel is real; moderate deployments, cite the paradigm, fix the two-senses divergence.** Both **agree** the cancer-cell/scale-consistency clause is genuinely refutable. They split on the deceptive channel — and here the critic scores a **real new formal catch** (the two senses of "coherence" come apart at the saving example) that the apologist's Berk framing doesn't address. Net: the *core* "optimal collapsers outperform" **is** analytic (critic right); the *content* lives in the two failure regimes + the *comparative* claim that real coherent systems **approximate** t\* (apologist right — and that comparative framing, à la marginal-value-theorem, is a genuine upgrade the book should claim). *Fix: (a) acknowledge the core is analytic and locate the content in the failure regimes + the comparative-benchmark claim; (b) **cite Charnov / marginal-value-theorem / ideal-observer** as the validated home (the apologist's gift); (c) resolve the two-senses-of-coherence divergence at the deceptive channel (the critic's catch); (d) moderate the confident deployments — marriages, paradigms — per generalist **B5**.*

### 4. The four conditions — independently operationalizable?

### 5. Moral facts as order-parameters (the physics half of the metaethics §4 fight)
- **Critic:** decorative — none of the four Landau ingredients (no order-parameter field, no symmetry group, no control parameter, no free energy); makes no prediction "settled vs. contested convention" doesn't. Commits the exact error the book prosecutes in IIT.
- **Apologist:** **Landau-level is a legitimate level** (a phenomenological theory, one of physics' most successful); the fix is to **name the implicit symmetry — permutation-invariance over navigators** — and recast the co-constitutive floor as the *symmetry-respecting invariants*; position it as the interpretive complement to the statistical-physics-of-social-dynamics program (Galam, Castellano–Fortunato–Loreto). It predicts a real *dynamical signature* (rigidity, hysteresis, critical flips at moral revolutions).
- **Verdict — CONVERGE on the fix + the panel's deepest cross-domain link.** Both agree the current text is *not* a cashed physics claim. The apologist supplies the constructive path (name the symmetry) — and **naming the symmetry as permutation-invariance over navigators *is* the impartiality premise the metaethics-critic said was smuggled (A1).** The physics-apologist's hardening and the ethics-critic's deepest objection are **the same point from opposite sides**: impartiality is a *posited symmetry*, not a corollary of the theorem. *Fix: either build the Z₂/permutation-invariance schema and own its predictions, or demote "order-parameter of broken symmetry" → "settled vs. contested phase" (a metaphor, not a theorem) — and see it as A1's physics face.*
