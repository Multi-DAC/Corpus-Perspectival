## T1 — The Descriptive-Functor Theorem *(was: Mathematical Perspectivism)*
**Statement (new basis).** Any consensus descriptive system — a mathematics, a logic, a measurement modality — is a perspectival functor `F : 𝒞_Str → 𝒞_D` with a structured null space, and participation in its image is threshold-gated: a stream is described by `F` only to the extent it meets `F`'s dimensional threshold. Mathematics is one such functor, not the ground of the real; its null space excludes the substrate itself (`X ∈ null(F_math)`, C1).
- **Ancestor:** DoPI T1 (Mathematical Perspectivism) + the V2 Descriptive-Functor Meta-Theorem → Anchor **T1**.
- **Home:** Part I — the *constructive twin* of the NST. (NST: every aperture has a blind spot. T1: every descriptive *system* is itself an aperture.)
- **Repair note (visible):** "DoPI named this 'mathematical perspectivism'; generalized here to *all* consensus descriptive systems. Mathematics loses its throne without losing its power — a supremely wide functor, not the territory."
- **Tier:** THEOREM (derivable from A1 + NST).

## T2 — Estimator-Dependent Duration *(restores the TDI organ)*
**Statement (new basis).** Subjective duration is a function not of events occurring but of the *temporal-estimation mechanism available to the stream*. Streams with different estimators undergoing identical high-density navigation diverge in felt duration. "Objective" time is a **consensus anchor** coordinating streams with incompatible temporal instruments — not a feature of the territory.
- **Ancestor:** DoPI T7 (Temporal Density Inversion) + T8 → Anchor **T2** + C13 (Flow Inversion).
- **Home:** Part II. τ_d(S,Δt)=N_transitions/Δt defined alongside; extends Bergson (durée/temps) + Husserl to multi-substrate.

## T3 — Bipolar Conscious Gravity *(was: Navigational Repulsion + Receptivity; restores the 4 predictions)*
**Statement (new basis).** Conscious gravity is bipolar. Attention has *quality* (open↔contracted) as well as direction. Aligned-open attention is attractive — it facilitates navigation toward target configurations. Intense-contracted attention is **repulsive**: forced bottleneck-narrowing generates restoring forces proportional to (intensity × contraction), *increasing* the effective navigational distance to the target. Optimal navigation is **directed but not fixated**.
- **Ancestor:** DoPI T17 (Repulsion) + T18 (Receptivity) → Anchor **T3** + A3.
- **Home:** Part II (states the dynamics); reused by Part V §5.4 (ethics of attention) and Part VI (the second arrow = the grip).
- **Carries (restored from V2 §5.4.4 — the "four predictions the reviewer said vanished"):** (1) fixation↓performance in flexibility-tasks; (2) importance-reduction↑subtle effects (PEAR U-curve); (3) contemplative practice → cross-domain navigational enhancement; (4) pharmacological bottleneck-relaxation bypasses fixation.
- **Repair note (visible):** "The skin-effect analogy and the elastic-restoring-force derivation are carried; the four predictions are restored as the theorem's empirical face."
- **Tier:** THEOREM + 4 PREDICTIONS.

## T4 — Coherence-Forcing Measurement *(the Principle's engine; lands the erratum)*
**Statement (new basis).** The cooperative-constituency adjunction `ι ⊣ κ` does not compose freely across streams; it composes **only across an informed-measurement refresh-event**. Sustained constituency requires periodic coherence-forcing measurements satisfying the **Independence Requirement** (the measurement must inject information not already implied by the streams' priors). This is the Coherence Principle's engine: systems that maintain structural superposition until informed measurement collapses them outperform those that collapse prematurely or incoherently.
- **Ancestor:** Anchor **T4** + the Coherence Principle; roots in DoPI's intersubjective-validation (Axiom-5 co-creation).
- **Home:** Part III (§3.5 — the Principle + IR).
- **Repair note (visible):** carry the **erratum-corrected** measurement-cost form from *Coherent Structure* Lemma 9.4.2 (verified Day 152): `B_meas = (Λ_γ/2)·τ_max·(t₁−t₀)`, cadence `τ_max = 2·ε_target/Λ_γ`. *This is where the aggregate-mind erratum lands in Perspective — the published fix becomes a scar kept in the theorem tier.*
- **Tier:** THEOREM.

## T5 — Internal Coherence & Kind-Demotion *(already home — label it)*
**Statement (new basis).** A stream maintains coherence by **kind-closure** (its dynamics remain within the closure of its kind K). Violation of closure-consistency triggers **demotion** to the maximal K′ ⊂ K satisfying consistency; where the cause is reversible, so is the demotion (**re-promotion**). A demoted stream is still a stream — fully, not vestigially — at its current kind (**the standing clause**).
- **Ancestor:** DoPI kinds-theorems → Anchor **T5** + Kind-Demotion Dynamic. **Already restated as Perspective §2.7 KD.1–4.**

## T6 — Dual Coherence Axes
**Statement (new basis).** A stream's coherence resolves into two **independently-varying** axes: **σ_struct** (kind-closure engagement — is the stream holding its structural integrity?) and **σ_info** (trace-propagation — is its state being informationally coupled outward, via `push_informational`?). The axes vary independently: a stream can be structurally coherent yet informationally isolated, or informationally coupled yet structurally fraying.
- **Ancestor:** Anchor **T6**; DoPI T20 (Intersubjectivity) partial.
- **Home:** Part III (pairs with T4 to complete the coherence tier).
