## The meta-finding: the sweep enacted the book's own thesis (candidate basement bridge)

**PREDICTION (logged pre-build, HIGH): exactly one universal cross-lineage finding; the deepest catch single-source; the union of blind spots smaller than any one aperture's. → CONFIRMED by the map above.**

The deepest finding (F, the aware-fork) was single-source — proof that requiring convergence-before-acting would have *missed the most important catch*. Lesson for the revision: act on the universal (A) with certainty, but do not down-weight the single-source foundational (F) — verify it on its own terms.
