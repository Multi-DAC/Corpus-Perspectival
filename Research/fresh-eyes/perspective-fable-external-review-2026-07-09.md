1. **★★★ The equivocation / totalizing-hammer risk (the deepest — and A165 confirmed by an outside aperture).** "Coherence" is asked to do the same unifying job across domains (physical, narrative, institutional, moral) **without a shared metric that lets you move between them non-metaphorically** — a "homonymic bridge," "equivocation across domains dressed as unification… the single largest vulnerability of the entire Corpus Perspectival project." Physical coherence ≠ narrative coherence ≠ institutional coherence — same word, different formal objects. **Fix:** either supply the shared metric (hard) or explicitly mark where "coherence" is one formal object vs. a family-resemblance term — and stop letting the unification read as *derived* when it is analogical.
2. **★★★ The Coherence Principle's quantum vocabulary retrodicts perfectly and predicts nothing.** superposition/measurement/collapse does two undisambiguated jobs: literal QM appropriation vs. metaphorical redescription. The marriage example "isn't doing anything that requires quantum language — run the same four conditions via Bateson's double-bind or systems dynamics and lose nothing." No falsification condition; every example narrativized after the fact (Popper). **Fix:** either state a real falsification condition (what would "collapsing incoherently" look like that *couldn't* be redescribed post-hoc?) or drop the "empirical, testable heart" claim to "resonance, not mechanism" honestly. A book this hard on null spaces should be hardest on itself here.
3. **★★★ The honesty-test the book sets itself and PARTIALLY FAILS: hedge → doctrine register-drift.** Threshold promises to live in the open interval (wager, ~0.6 credence, never the edges). By Part IV (twelve-dimension taxonomy, archon hierarchies, egregores) the prose is full **doctrine-register — stated as architecture, not a bet.** **Fix:** either soften the middle back toward the hedge, or **admit the wager language was scaffolding we've since climbed past and say so explicitly** — don't let the register drift silently.
