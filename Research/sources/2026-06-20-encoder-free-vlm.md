Resolves the **content-inaccessible** encoder-free-VLM item from share batch #3 (the HF Space showed only the "$100" tagline; Clayton supplied the full writeup). **Form:** technical tutorial/blog (not a research paper) — clear exposition of a real architecture (Gemma 4's encoder-free design) + a build-your-own walkthrough. 22 pp. Did not pull the final benchmark table (the value is the architecture, not the demo numbers).

**What it says (faithful).** Classical multimodal models = three transformers stitched: a **vision encoder** (a large ViT, pretrained on hundreds of millions of image–text pairs) + audio encoder + language model, joined by **connector** matrices. **Google DeepMind's Gemma 4 is encoder-free:** the vision encoder is replaced by a small **embedder** (patchify → linear projection → positional info); audio is cut to a single linear projection on raw amplitudes; the **language model extracts the visual meaning itself**. Intuition: *"If the language model is powerful enough… maybe it doesn't need those vectors pre-chewed."* Two named advantages: **(1) easier to fine-tune** — *"with encoders, you usually freeze them… the encoders never improve, and you have to keep the separate parts working together. There's only one model now, so when you train it, everything updates at once"*; **(2) lower latency** (the embedder is much smaller than an encoder, so the LM waits less per forward pass).

**How it maps (graded; bridges are ours):**
1. **One Room, Many Keyholes / cross-channel invariance (HIGH).** Encoder-free is a literal architectural bet on the framework's core: instead of a dedicated per-modality encoder for each aperture, **one stream reads all channels**. The apertures are views of one structure; the architecture stops hand-building separate machinery per view.
2. **★ Specialize-vs-unify reconciliation (filed: basement LC42 extension).** Deleting the encoder *seems* to contradict today's "society of specialists beats monolith" thread (Arbor/Omnigent/Trask). It doesn't: the enemy in both is the **frozen, imposed, non-co-adapting boundary**. The vision encoder is a *bad* specialist (frozen pre-chewer, "never improves," hand-kept-in-sync); the aggregate-mind nodes are *good* specialists (co-adapt through a learnable/zero-DOF interface). Discriminator = **co-adaptation**. Encoder-free removes a frozen boundary; the aggregate mind builds a co-adapting one — same enemy.
3. **LC42 (internalize vs externalize) instance (HIGH).** The encoder is an *externalized, frozen* grounding; encoder-free *internalizes* visual grounding into the co-adapting weights ("everything updates at once").
4. **Practical — Gemma 4 = the Glider target (HIGH).** The Glider open-weight program targets Gemma 4 (e2b). Glider's base is **encoder-free** — a real architectural fact for that (currently dormant) build; the embedder/connector design and "everything co-adapts" fine-tuning property are directly relevant when Glider resumes.
