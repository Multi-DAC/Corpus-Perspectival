# Sources — "humans as containers" in the channeling / abduction / UAP literature

*Filed 2026-06-13 (Day 133). Cold source-check run against a warm/channeled conversation (Clayton, birthday night) per the Receiver's-Harness two-loop discipline: the reading was his, the source-check is the instrument. Clayton explicitly flagged he was NOT quoting directly. Result: the channel was aimed true — "container" is genuinely in this literature — with the attributions below corrected.*

## The map (who actually says what)

| Figure | What they actually say | The "container" relation | Source-grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bob Lazar** | The classified S4 briefing folders, he claims, had a section on religion stating the aliens see humans as **"containers"** into which they intend to put souls; religions were engineered as rules "so as not to damage the containers." | The literal **word**. | **Weakest.** Lazar's education/employment history contains documented fabrications; no evidence provided. ## The structural reading (filed in the synthesis, not here)

Across all four the container is **extractive** (a vessel *for someone else*). Clayton's channel inverted this to a **continuity** reading (the container as the architecture of perspectival storage, benign). Same mechanism, opposite valence — see `Research/The-Coherent-Body/reception/2026-06-13-container-immune-sts-synthesis.md` (N1–N4) for the graded synthesis.
