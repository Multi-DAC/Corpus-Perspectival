## The claims, by kind

1. **DMT entity cartography.** On DMT: a large, distinct array of "species" — insectoids, reptilians, jokers, plus angelic/demonic — that *coexist and follow rules* ("all on a bus together"). Standard McKenna/Strieber/Mack/Gallimore lineage taxonomy.

3. **Active-inference framing.** *"The eyes are not working like a camera… the brain is guessing outward, building world models."* Friston/Clark predictive processing, arrived at independently.
4. **★ The Vision Pro claim (title hook).** On DMT you see moving "code" in a *band of light* on the wall. They photograph the band at high resolution, clean it, load the *static* image into the Apple Vision Pro (VR); then, smoking DMT while viewing it, "the code appears in the image" and *moves*. Plus a **brain-scan apparatus** (with Chris Paris + "Sterling"): click where you see a symbol → registers the EEG/scan at that moment (±5 s) + the spatial location → a dataset mapping subjective symbol-sightings to brain-state + place.
5. **Self-described falsifiable.** He claims the framework is "testable and falsifiable — there would be things that, if we saw them, would render it completely false." (Does not specify what; credit the instinct, rare in this space.)

The claim would only bite if **sober, independent observers decoded the same structure from the static photo** — which is not claimed. Verdict: not yet distinguished from pareidolia-on-texture + DMT motion-overlay.

## The constructive through-line (what's actually worth doing)

Strip the overclaim and the Vision Pro idea points at a *real* experiment we'd know how to design: **does the captured static image carry cross-subject mutual information above a texture/noise baseline?** Take the high-res photo of the "band of light"; have N *sober* observers independently annotate/decode structure; test whether their decodings agree above what the same protocol yields on matched control textures (JPEG-artifact fields, sensor-noise fields, moiré). If yes → a genuinely novel, non-cultural datum (something *was* captured). If no → it's the perceiver, as expected. That converts Goler's instinct into a falsification surface with the perceiver-confound removed — the exact move the book teaches (instrument the phenomenology; keep the failure condition external).
