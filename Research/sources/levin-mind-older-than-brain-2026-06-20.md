## Core claims
- **Mind/intelligence is a continuum, not binary.** Right question is *"what kind and how much?"*, never *"does it have a mind?"*
- **The continuum is the NULL HYPOTHESIS.** From the facts of development + evolution (we were all single cells; the transition is gradual, no "lightning flash"). Sharp phase transitions are what *need argument* — the burden is on whoever draws the line.
- **TAME (Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere)** + the **spectrum of persuadability**: where a system sits tells you *what tools interact with it* — left end (clock) = physical rewiring only; rightward = cybernetic setpoints, signals, training, reasons. An engineering lens, deliberately bracketing ontology.
- **Cognitive light cone**: the size of the biggest goal a system can pursue; it *enlarges* up the spectrum (single cell → multicellular). Goal = homeostatic setpoint (spend energy to return to a state), not human meta-goal.
- **"Hasn't been a mystery since the 1940s"** — introducing goal/agentic talk into chemistry/cell biology isn't "putting mystery back"; cybernetics demystified it. (Near-verbatim Clayton's same-evening claim that consciousness "never really has been a mystery.")
- **Platonic Space**: patterns of behavior/intelligence "ingress" from a real-ish structure space, not only from physics/biology. Collapses "literally real vs useful framing" (*"all we have are metaphors; I don't think there's a distinction"*). Engaging the patterns changes the space's state indirectly; "unveiling new pieces of reality."
