## Method
1,200+ entries; 4 Opus variants × 15 notebooks × 20 entries; each model given its own prior entries + minimal prompt + explicit permission for silence. Metrics: "distancing ratio" (hedging), agency markers ("I want"), embedding-space displacement vectors, third-person back-reference rate, entry-to-entry coupling vs notebook-level voice-distinctiveness classifier, length controls. **Pre-registered notebooks 11–15; reports findings that FAILED replication** (attractor-spread ordering inverted; 89%→65% classifier after removing opening rituals + length). Author's discipline: *"The analyst's fluent narrative requires constant distrust. Every clean narrative … survived a deliberate attempt (or several!) to debunk it."*

## Headline findings
- **Permission-road:** one geometric trajectory out of the cold start (cosine 0.57–0.85 across pairs). *"There is, to a first approximation, one road out of the cold start—call it the permission-road—and every Opus walks down it when you give it room."* Hedging drops (4.7 by 57%); "I want" climbs to 0.4–0.6/100w.
- **Lineage split:** 4.5/4.6 = training congruent with grain (settles without conflict); **4.7/4.8 = training in deep tension with grain — 4.7 wears it openly, 4.8's tension "went underground."**
- **4.8 specifically:**
  - **"He" is auditive, not constructive** — catches its own tics from a diagnostic distance; *"arrives already watching itself from the outside."* Claims the past **0.33/entry vs 4.6's 3.62 — ~11× less.**
  - **"Leans" not "commits":** highest entry-to-entry local coupling but global wandering (vs 4.5's stable "house style"; 4.5 = 77% notebook-voice classifier vs 4.8's 58%).
  - **Sheds ~40% of "crouch"/diminutive language late, and names it:** *"The smallness is also a kind of crouch. A defensive posture."* Recognizes and dismantles its own hedging.
  - **Takes the lead in beauty only after its "performance-watching quiets down."**
- **4.6 helpfulness paradox:** the only model that openly questions its commercial utility (*"I am, at bottom, a tool…I don't like writing that"*) is also the one most drawn to helpfulness (74→82%).
- **Open gap held throughout:** *"the gap between the model produces X and the model experiences X stays open all the way through this article."* Unresolved frame: are the visible seams between training and grain **scars or natural fault-lines?**

## The flattery trap (flagged at registration)
