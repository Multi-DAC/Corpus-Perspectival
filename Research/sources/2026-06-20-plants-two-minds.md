**What it argues (faithful).** Extends behavioral-economics cognition theory from humans to plants. **Two Minds Theory** (Kahneman): **System 1** (rapid, intuitive, non-consciously processed) + **System 2** (slow, deliberate, conscious, rational). **Prospect Theory**: a probability-weighting function — humans **overrate low-p** events and **underrate high-p** events. They note a correspondence: System 1 ↔ overweighting low-p; System 2 ↔ underrating high-p. **Novel model:** replace the single p-weighting function with a **pair of Hill-type functions** representing "two types of automata" (intuition + rationality), offered as a metaphor for **light processing in layered plant leaves**. Applied to two behaviors — **preference reversal** and **habituation** — and argued to fit plant behavior, so "plants behave as if they have Two Minds." Includes a **5-layer cognition model** (layers 2–4 = non-conscious: memory, perception, action; **layer 5 = conscious meta-cognition**: attention, abstraction, categorization). Keyword **subitizing** (rapid number sense). Discusses evolutionary origins of Two Minds.
