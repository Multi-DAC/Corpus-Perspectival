**Ref:** Twan Hooijschuur, Ehsan Irani, Antoine Deblais, Sara Jabbari-Farouji. arXiv:2509.21599v2 [cond-mat.soft], Oct 2025. Univ. Amsterdam / Göttingen.

## What it is / key findings
Large-scale 3D Brownian-dynamics of tangentially-driven active semiflexible polymers (bead-spring chains; vary density ρσ³, activity fᵃ, bending stiffness κ). Findings:
- **Activity systematically shifts the isotropic–nematic (I–N) transition to higher densities**, set by flexibility + activity.
- **Activity changes the NATURE/order of the transition:** discontinuous (first-order) at low activity → **continuous** at moderate → **suppressed** at high activity.
- **Mechanism:** activity enhances collective bending fluctuations → chain shrinkage → enlarged effective confinement tube (reff ≫ geometric rρ) → delays the confinement-induced stretching that drives nematic order in passive systems.
- **⭐ Moderate-activity INSTABILITY (the striking feature):** stochastic switching between globally-ordered nematic and globally-disordered states (Fig 3: SB oscillates nematic↔disordered on ~10τD, director reorients to a new axis); even when global order vanishes, LOCAL nematic order persists. An activity-induced instability of the nematic field.
- Stiffness modulates: stiffer → transition at lower density; rod-limit κ=128 stays continuous/stable up to ρσ³=1.0, fᵃ=2.0. I–N onset coincides with bend-over-splay deformation predominance; activity delays it.
