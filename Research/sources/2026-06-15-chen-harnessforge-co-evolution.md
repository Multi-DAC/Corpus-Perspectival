# HarnessForge: Joint Harness and Policy Evolution for Adaptive Agent Systems

- **Authors:** Mingju Chen, Can Lv, Guibin Zhang, Heng Chang, Shiji Zhou
- **arXiv:** 2606.01779 (2026)
- **Shared by:** Clayton, 2026-06-15 (Day 135) — supplied as the "Chen et al. (2026)" citation for the TMI *Steerable Coherence* grant proposal.

## What it is
Framework for **co-evolving** an LLM agent's *harness* (execution structure) and its *policy* (reasoning behavior) jointly, rather than adapting either in isolation. Optimizes the compatibility between external system components and internal reasoning across heterogeneous task regimes. Reports up to ~+12% over baselines.
