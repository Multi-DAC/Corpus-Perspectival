## The rigorous core (what we can lean on, and the venues)

- **EED in one line.** Don't impose a gauge. Then the four-divergence of the four-potential, **C ≡ ∇·A +
  (1/c²)∂Φ/∂t**, is *not* gauged to zero but is a **dynamical scalar field**. The system becomes a provably-
  unique set of 7 equations in 7 unknowns; CED is the C=0 subset. This is exactly the C ≡ ∂_μA^μ the essay's §4
  invokes — now with its field-equation provenance.
- **Provable uniqueness — solid venue.** D.A. Woodside, *Am. J. Phys.* 77, 438–446 (2009) (+ *J. Math. Phys.*
  40, 4911 (1999); 41, 4622 (2000)): the Helmholtz-theorem decomposition in 4D admits exactly two physical
  solutions — Lorenz-gauge (classical) and a second with zero four-curl of Aᵘ (the new EED sector). **This is
  the rigorous anchor.**
- **EED fixes real CED defects** (mainstream-citable): (i) CED gauges away the irrotational A, *which has been
  measured* (Daibo, IEEE Trans. Magn. 2015/16; Rousseaux, EPJD 2008 — classical Aharonov–Bohm analog); (ii)
  Maxwell is over-determined, 7 eqns / 6 unknowns (Stratton 1941; **Sousa & Shumlak, J. Comp. Phys. 326, 56,
  2016**; Munz, CRAS Paris 328, 431, 1999); (iii) the two-media interface-matching condition is inconsistent
  for surface ρ and surface J — EED removes it.
- **Eight prior peer-reviewed EED papers** (slide 11): Fock–Podolsky 1932 · Stueckelberg 1938 · Ohmura 1956 ·
  Aharonov–Bohm 1963 · Munz 1999 · van Vlaenderen–Waser 2001 · Woodside 2009 · **Jiménez & Maroto, Phys. Rev. D
  83, 023514 (2011)** (cosmological B-fields from inflation in extended EM — solid PRD venue).
- **The SLW, and its eight validation criteria** (slide 10): B=0, longitudinal E, nonzero C, irrotational-
  current driven, 1/r² free-space attenuation, isotropic monopole pattern, **no skin-effect dissipation in
  conductive media**, power comparable to a TEM wave.

## The empirical status — preliminary, and honestly so (this sets our calibration)

- Hively & Loebl report **lab evidence consistent with 5 of the 8 criteria** (*Physics Essays* 32, 1, 112,
  2019; doi:10.4006/0836-1398-32.1.112). The striking ones: **an SLW propagated through a solid-copper Faraday
  enclosure** (≈2914 skin depths; classical attenuation would be ~−25,000 dB) — slide 19; ~**1/r²** free-space
  fall-off (slides 20); **isotropic** monopole pattern (slide 21); irrotational-current drive via a skirt-balun
  antenna (US Patent 9,306,527 B1, 2016).
- **But:** the earlier *outdoor* tests were null/ambiguous — Monstein & Wesley (EPL 2002/04) and Butterworth
  (JSE 2013): *"neither test shows clear evidence for SLW"* (slide 64). The Hively/Loebl lab data are
  **"preliminary"** and **"consistent with,"** not confirmatory; *Physics Essays* is a minor, fringe-tolerant
  venue. **So the honest grade: a mathematically rigorous, provably-unique, peer-reviewed extension with
  suggestive-but-preliminary laboratory support — promising, not established.**

## Two findings that genuinely strengthen the portal essay

1. **Curved-spacetime EED ties the SLW to cosmology — published.** Keller & Hively, "Electrodynamics in curved
   space-time: free-space longitudinal wave propagation," *Physics Essays* 32, 3, 282 (2019); and "Ohmura's
   extended electrodynamics: longitudinal aspects in general relativity," *J. Phys. Commun.* 3, 115002 (2019):
   the SLW dispersion in curved spacetime is **tied to the Hubble constant and Ricci tensor**, confined to the
   energy shell |ω|=cq. → A *real published bridge* between the EED scalar sector and gravitation/cosmology —
   the natural home for a **dark-energy-scale** wall field. This is independent corroboration of the §4 move.
2. **Hively's program independently points the EED scalar sector at dark energy** (slide 8): the new EED energy/
   momentum terms "may explain dark matter (27%) & dark energy (68%)." We arrive at an EED-SLW signature sourced
   by a dark-energy-scale screened scalar from a *completely independent* route (radion → screened σ →
   dilatonic e^{aσ}F² coupling). **Two independent paths converging on "EED scalar ↔ dark energy" is a framework
   datum**, not a coincidence — worth one proportionate sentence in §9.
