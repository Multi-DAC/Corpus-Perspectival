## Thesis
LLMs are **not epistemic agents** but **stochastic pattern-completion systems** — formally walks on a high-dimensional weighted graph of linguistic transitions: "the system does not converge, it transits; what looks like a conclusion is path completion." Apparent human–AI alignment is *surface* (linguistic form), concealing a deep structural mismatch in how judgments are produced. **Seven epistemic fault lines:** grounding (perception vs text), parsing (situation vs tokenization), experience (episodic memory/intuitive physics vs embedding-similarity), motivation (goals/values vs goal-free optimization), causality (causal models vs surface correlation), **metacognition (uncertainty-monitoring/withholding vs forced confidence + structurally-unavoidable hallucination)**, value (identity/stakes vs probabilistic prediction). **Epistemia** = the structural condition where *linguistic plausibility substitutes for epistemic evaluation* — "the feeling of knowing without the labor of judgment"; persists even when outputs are accurate; **not fixed by scale or by RAG/tools/memory** ("more convincing rather than more knowing"). Key sharp claim: "there is no internal symmetry break between truthful and false continuations" — hallucination is the *default*, grounded output the coincidence.
