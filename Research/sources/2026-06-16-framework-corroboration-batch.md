## A — Extended / higher-derivative electrodynamics (backs §4 "EED is taken seriously now")

1. **Singh & Kumar, "Plasma Dynamics in Higher-Derivative Electrodynamics: A Renormalised Two-Loop Framework,"
   arXiv 2508.01665 (Aug 2025).** Bopp–Podolsky (higher-derivative ED) in electron–proton plasma; no new
   divergences at two loops, double-Yukawa inter-particle force, intrinsic UV completion of electrostatics.
   → EED ⊗ plasma, peer-track. **Citable in §4 / Appendix A** as current EED-in-plasma work.
2. **Shorba, Mahmoud & Abdelhamid, "Incompressible Extended Magnetohydrodynamics Waves: Implications of Electron
   Inertia," arXiv 2605.10697 (May 2026).** XMHD dispersion (Hall + electron inertia); distinct ion-cyclotron
   & whistler branches saturating at the gyrofrequencies. → extended-MHD; supports the "extended" framing of
   the carrier's plasma dynamics (R10/R11).
10. **Bajardi, Benetti, Capozziello & Dib, "Extended Theories of Electrodynamics in f(R) Gravity," arXiv
    2603.16979 (Mar 2026).** Couples EM curvature invariants to f(R) gravity; **recovers Bopp–Podolsky +
    Plebanski models as limits**; phenomenology in extreme/early-universe/compact-object regimes. → EED ⊗
    gravity ⊗ (f(R) = dark-energy-adjacent). The single most on-theme paper: extended-ED *inside a
    modified-gravity/dark-energy frame* — our exact intersection. **Strong §4 citation candidate.**
14. **"Dielectric and conductive properties of solids: classical versus extended electrodynamics"
    (ResearchGate 390766250; 403 on fetch — confirm venue/authors).** EED predictions for dielectric/conductive
    response of solids vs classical ED — i.e. a **testable lab signature of EED in matter**. Directly supports
    the §7 program (lab tests of the EED/SLW sector). **Read + cite candidate.**
17. **Annals of Physics, S0003491625003689 (2025; 403 on fetch — confirm title/authors).** Likely an EED or
    plasma theory paper given the cluster. To read.
