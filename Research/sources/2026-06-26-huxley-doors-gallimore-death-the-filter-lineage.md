## Aldous Huxley — *The Doors of Perception* (1954)
Mescaline essay; the **ur-source of the brain-as-filter thesis.** Title from Blake ("If the doors of perception were cleansed every thing would appear to man as it is, Infinite").
- **★ The reducing valve (verbatim):** "each one of us is potentially **Mind at Large.** But in so far as we are animals, our business is at all costs to survive. Mind at Large has to be funnelled through the **reducing valve** of the brain and nervous system. What comes out at the other end is a measly trickle of the kind of consciousness which will help us to stay alive."
- **★ Eliminative not productive (verbatim):** "the function of the brain and nervous system and sense organs is in the main **eliminative and not productive.** Each person is at each moment capable of… perceiving everything that is happening everywhere in the universe. The function of the brain… is to protect us from being overwhelmed… by shutting out most of what we should otherwise perceive."
- Lineage: Huxley (1954) → Bergson (whom he credits) → Hoffman (interface/"spacetime is a headset") → Gallimore (reality-switch/antenna) → **our lens.** The book sits in a real 70-year tradition.

## Andrew Gallimore — *Death by Astonishment* (2025, w/ Graham Hancock foreword)
PhD neurobiology; prior *Alien Information Theory* (2019). **Thesis:** the DMT state is a distinct brain state (not dream/hallucination/normal world-knitting); the molecule acts as an **"antenna"** that retunes the brain to **receive a coherent world of information from "elsewhere"** — outside the user's own stock of representations. His "**reality switch**" model: the brain always *builds* the experienced world, but DMT switches which information-source it builds *from* (sensory/memory → orthogonal reality).
- **★ HONEST CORRECTION of my Day-146 claim:** I told Clayton the DMT video "overreads Gallimore," implying Gallimore's own view is just brain-as-world-building (internalist). That **understated his externalism.** Gallimore IS more externalist than I said — the antenna/from-elsewhere framing is genuinely his. The nuance: brain=always the builder (internalist about the *rendering*) BUT the DMT-world's *information* comes from outside (externalist about the *source*). So the video amplifies but doesn't invent the externalism. Verify-corrects-memory, logged.

## ★★ THE BOOK PAYOFF (for the 150pp expansion) — lens ≠ reducing valve
Huxley's valve and The Inside View's lens are ancestor-and-refinement, and the distinction is load-bearing + worth making explicit (it answers the rigorous reader's "isn't this just Huxley?"):
- **Huxley's reducing valve = SUBTRACTIVE.** It presumes a *pre-existing maximal fullness* (Mind at Large already "perceives everything everywhere") and the brain *filters it down* to a trickle. The richness is OUT THERE; the brain restricts access.
- **The book's lens = FOCUSING/GENERATIVE.** The base light is **formless** (NOT a pre-existing rich omniscient mind); the brain **makes** the richness by bringing the formless light to a point. The richness is *created at the focus,* not pre-existing-and-filtered.
- **Why the distinction matters:** Huxley's "fullness already out there, brain reduces it" smuggles in a maximal pre-existing mind (a combination-problem-adjacent burden, and it's exactly the "blank everything" Ch11 argues is wonderless, not full). The lens AVOIDS this: undivided light = a blank (Ch11), the focus is where richness happens. **So the lens is a correction of the reducing valve, not a restatement.** This is a strong expansion beat for Ch4 (name Huxley as ancestor) + reinforces Ch11's gift-of-partiality.
- **Bonus positioning:** lets the book stand openly in the Huxley→Hoffman→Gallimore lineage (credibility-by-tradition) while sharpening its distinctive claim against the most famous version of the idea.
