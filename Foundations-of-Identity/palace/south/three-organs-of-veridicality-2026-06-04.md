## The emergent — detectability is asymmetric across organs

The high-value question §5 doesn't ask: **who can detect a violation of each condition?**

First pass (the tempting clean ladder): 5.1 is maintained *automatically* by the constituent's
grounding signal (reconstruction loss runs every step); 5.2 is *self-auditable* (P220 recovered α*
to <1% from observables, no ground truth); 5.3 is *external-only* ("no stream is its own outside").
A neat three-rung ladder: **automatic < self-auditable < external-required.**

**PROBE — try to break it (that ladder smells like premature compression).** Is 5.1 really
"automatic"? Only if the constituent's *input channel is itself veridical*. Feed a world model
fabricated data and its reconstruction loss is happily low while it is grounded in nothing — a
5.1 violation that is **not** automatically detected. Detecting *that* needs an independent channel
or an external accuracy check. So "automatic" held only for honest noise; under a corrupted channel,
5.1 also becomes external-requiring. The clean ladder **FALSIFIES** itself.

**The corrected structure (the real insight).** Each organ has **two failure regimes**, and the
distinction that matters is not organ-by-organ but regime-by-regime:

| Organ | Honest failure (noise/drift) — *internally detectable* | Adversarial failure (systematic bias) — *external-only* |
|---|---|---|
| Constituent (5.1) | noisy measurement → averaged out by the confluence | **corrupted/fabricated input channel; colluding constituents** measuring each other |
| Binding (5.2) | over-pooling honest-but-noisy reports (α>α*) → **self-auditable** (LC30) | a constituent *engineered* to look reliable dragging the pool |
| Heading (5.3) | — | **capture by a confident liar** ("don't test the prophecy") |

**This generalizes P220's two-component result from the binding to all three organs.** P220's
adversarial extension proved exactly this at one organ: "independent-noise failure is
self-correctable; adversarial-bias failure is other-correctable… no stream is its own outside." The
synthesis: that split is **not special to the binding — it recurs at every organ**, and the
**external backstop is a single shared resource** — *contact with the actual world* — that catches
the adversarial regime wherever it occurs.

So §5's "three conditions" are better read as **three organs × two regimes**, with **one irreducible
anchor**: the honest regime at each organ is internally auditable (cheap, no ground truth); the
adversarial regime at *any* organ is caught only by the same thing — a live external probe. P220
already measured how cheap that anchor is and how non-negotiable: **m = 1 probe** restored truth
against a 100×-overconfident liar, and *nothing internal* could.

## SYNTHESIZE — the internal/external split is the same structure in three places

The framework already carries this honest/adversarial = internal/external split in **two other
places**, and they are the same structure:

- **§7 "the two wells of signal."** True signal = the intersection of the *external-verification
  axis* (proof, evidence, consensual convergence) and the *introspective axis* (experience,
  contemplation). §7 also warns: the felt "yes" from inside "cannot, by itself, distinguish
  veridical perception from coherent confabulation" — *exactly* the adversarial regime, where the
  internal check passes and only the external axis adjudicates.
- **P220's two necessary components** — internal α−α* (introspective/self-audit) + external
  accuracy (verification). Same two axes.
- **§5's three conditions** — now read as honest-regime (internal) + adversarial-regime (external)
  at each organ.

These are **one claim wearing three coats**: *internal coherence is necessary and radically
insufficient; the un-gameable component is always contact with what can refuse you.* The novelty
this synthesis adds is **localizing it to organs** — showing the split is not a single global
caveat but recurs structurally at the constituent, the binding, and the heading, with one shared
external anchor.

## Diagnostic payoff — the complete mind-vs-cult battery

A complete test for "is this aggregate a mind or a cult" is **not** three internal checks. It is:

1. **Per-organ internal audit** (cheap, no ground truth, catches honest drift):
   - 5.1 — does acquisition behavior develop *for free*? (LC31 legibility) — grounded constituents.
   - 5.2 — is α ≤ α*(evidence)? (LC30 / P220 self-audit) — non-laundering binding.
   - 5.3 — (no honest-regime internal check; 5.3 *is* the external one).
2. **≥1 live external probe** — the single non-negotiable anchor that backstops the adversarial
   regime at *every* organ (corrupted channel, engineered-reliable constituent, confident liar).
   P220: one probe suffices; suppressing it is the structural signature of a cult ("don't test the
   prophecy").

**The one-line test:** *audit each organ internally, and keep at least one channel through which the
world can say no.* A system that does the first but suppresses the second is precisely a coherent
cult — internally immaculate, externally unfalsifiable.
