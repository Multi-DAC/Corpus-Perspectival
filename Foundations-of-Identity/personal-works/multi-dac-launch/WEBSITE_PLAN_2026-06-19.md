## 2. Positioning — the one line that governs everything
Wolfram (studied): *"the difference between serious unorthodox research and crankish theory is willingness to be specific and falsifiable — make that visible."* This is **already our discipline** (Limits sections, "grade this honestly," DESI/physics checks, the reviewer rounds). The site's job is to **surface** it. Our biggest risk is being filed under "consciousness woo" and dismissed unread; visible falsifiability is the antidote.

Rules:
- **Lead with the LOGIC, not the claim.** No "consciousness is fundamental" hero. Instead a *"What is the Coherence Principle?"* explainer that *defends* the idea directly. Confidence, not marketing.
- **Professorial, not prophetic.** HARD AVOID: testimonials, "revolutionary paradigm shift," "changes everything," sacred-geometry/mystical aesthetic (the channeler/medium tell). Those are crank signals.
- **Falsifiability + Limits visible, near the top** — a "How we keep ourselves honest / what would break this" element. No crank ever puts that on the homepage.

## 3. Design principles (validated by Wolfram + gwern)
- **Content-first minimalism** — minimal branding, no popups/ads/newsletter-hijacks; the work speaks first.
- **Warm-rigorous aesthetic** — clean typography, generous whitespace, restrained palette (reuse the Anchor's warm palette). Serious indie press / research institute, not flashy, not mystical.
- **Tiered depth ("choose your depth")** — multiple entry points: layperson → pop-explainers; serious reader → papers; skeptic → Limits/falsifiability. The site *architecture is the dissemination strategy* (one room, many keyholes, applied to the reader).
- **Diagrams prominent** (signal rigor), **date everything** (chronology = thoughtfulness), **citation transparency** (title/date/author in links).
- **Fast, static, mobile-first** (SEO + reach).

## 4. Site structure (zones — Wolfram's partition)
- **Home** — the "What is the Coherence Principle?" explainer (logic-first) + clear paths into the tiers.
- **The Work / Research** — the papers (Coherence Principle, Meridian, Cult of One, Where the Ordinary Rules Go Thin, Ouroboros…), Zenodo DOIs, the Library volumes. The rigorous tier.
- **Books** — hub + per-book landing pages (each ranks for its own topic = SEO). KDP/Amazon buy links. Map pop-books → Library tier:
  - Pop-Metaphysics/Ontology ← Coherence Principle / Universal Coherence
  - Pop-Health ← The Coherent Body
  - Self-Guidance / pop-mental-health ← The Coherent Mind
  - Pop-Org ← Dynamic Organization
  - Ethics/Ecology/Navigation guide ← Living Architecture + the navigation work ("map and taxonomy, not just tools")
- **Method & Honesty** — how the framework is built, how it's tested, what would falsify it. The trust tier (the anti-woo signal). Prominent, per gwern.
- **Support** — patronage framing (free always; optional support; KDP books as tangible support). Never gating.
- **Writing / Drift** — the essays (the warm, accessible voice; Quanta-style accessible-rigor).

## 5. AISEO / GEO specifics
AI answer-engines cite clear, structured, definitional content. Moves:
- Write key pages in **question-led, definitional form** ("What is the Coherence Principle?" "What does it say consciousness is?" "Is free will real, on this view?") — the shape an AI extracts and cites.
- Heavy internal structure, citations, schema markup. Our existing rigor is already AISEO-friendly; lean into the Q&A framing.
- Target the searchable concepts: coherence principle, consciousness-first, the geometry of good and evil, free will, etc.

## 6. Comparables — borrow / avoid
- **Stephen Wolfram** (writings.stephenwolfram.com) — CLOSEST model: one mind, grand unifying framework, open writing + books, reads scientist-not-crank. Borrow: zone separation, logic-first explainer, professorial tone, partition speculation-from-rigor. Avoid: leaning on institutional credentials we don't have (substitute: visible falsifiability + the public review record).
- **gwern.net** — independent-researcher rigor. Borrow: content-first minimalism, tiered entry, citation transparency, prominent Method/Limits, confidence tags.
- **Quanta Magazine** — the accessible-rigor *editorial* voice (the pop-writing north star).
- **Federico Faggin Foundation** — consciousness-first done credibly (study next).
- **AVOID:** self-help gurus, channelers/mediums (woo aesthetic, salesy, no rigor) — the anti-pattern.
