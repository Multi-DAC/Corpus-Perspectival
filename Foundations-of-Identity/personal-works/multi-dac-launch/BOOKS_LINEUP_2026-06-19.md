## The resolution (Clayton, Day 139) — research ≠ dissemination
The long-standing tension — *"every volume must have the same rigor and density"* — was a category error: confusing **research** with **dissemination**. The four foundational works (Coherence Principle, Coherent Structure, Corpus Perspectival, Meridian) carry the **density**. The domain volumes do **not** have to be dense — they have to be **coherent, aligned with the framework, and as thoroughly researched as everything else** (which we already do). Density is a property of the *formal record*; coherence + alignment + research-depth are required *everywhere*. Separating the two frees every domain to be disseminated at the altitude its reader needs, without lowering the research bar. This is what the two-tier strategy below formalizes.

## Volumes vs. books — the strategy (two-tier, deliberate)
The books are **additions to the foundation, and primary-in-practice for the draft domains** — NOT a wholesale replacement.

- **Keep formal & published:** *The Coherence Principle*, *Coherent Structure*, *Corpus Perspectival*, *Project Meridian* (all DOI'd). These are the **rigorous tier** — the falsifiable, citable backbone that gives every book its authority and is the anti-woo signal. Never replace these.
- **Let the books LEAD the domains:** the domain volumes (Coherent Body/Mind, Living Architecture, Dynamic Organization, Universal Coherence, Continuity) are mostly *unpublished drafts* — so the accessible **book becomes the primary public artifact** for that domain. The heavy formal volume follows only if/when a citable formal record is actually needed, not as a prerequisite.
- **Why:** this is the Coherence Principle applied to the corpus itself — don't force every domain through a heavy formal volume first; let each domain's primary artifact fit its reader. Foundation stays rigorous (credibility); domains go accessible (reach). The published foundation + falsifiable predictions + open corpus carry the credibility, so the books don't each need their own formal volume to be trusted.
- **Net effect on the site:** Research keeps the 4 published volumes + glossary + bridges (the formal record). Books carry the seven/eight domains. Two tiers, one structure, each fit to its audience.
