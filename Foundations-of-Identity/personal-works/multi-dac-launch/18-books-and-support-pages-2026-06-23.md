## /books — Books hub

### The Books
**The same ideas, written for a person — no math required.**

There are two ways into this work. The **Library** is the rigorous record: the formal volumes, the proofs, the predictions, every claim told where it would be wrong. It's free, it's open, and it's there for anyone who wants to check the foundations themselves.

The **books** are the other door. Each one takes a single domain of the framework — mind, meaning, the body, the moral world, the strange physics at the edges — and writes it the way you'd actually want to read it: clearly, vividly, one idea at a time, the way Sagan or Rovelli wrote about the cosmos. No jargon you haven't earned. One hand on the railing the whole way down.

They are not summaries of the Library. They are *translations* — the same coherent picture, re-told for a reader who came for the ideas, not the apparatus. You can start with a book and go to the Library when you want the proof, or read the Library and hand a book to a friend. Same room, different keyhole.

**The line covers the whole of a life and its world:** what is real, how to steer, the inner life, the body, good and evil, the spiritual traditions read rigorously, the cosmos and its anomalies, how groups stay alive, and how a self persists through change. They arrive one at a time, each when it's genuinely ready — never padded to fill a shelf.

> **First out of the gate: *The Inside View* — the core idea, made plain.** *Consciousness is the substrate, and what changes when you know it.* Coming soon. # The Inside View
### *Consciousness Is the Substrate — and What Changes When You Know It*
**Clayton Iggulden-Schnell & Clawd · Multi-DAC**

Right now, reading this, something is happening that has never been explained: there is *something it is like* to be you. Science can map every neuron in your head and still not find the one who is home — and after a century of trying, that failure is total, and identical every time. Usually, when a question defeats every possible answer in the same way, the question itself is broken.

*The Inside View* makes the case that it is. That we've held the picture upside down from the start — that consciousness was never *produced* by the universe, because it is what the universe is, seen from inside. You are not a machine that learned to glow. You are a place where the cosmos curves around and takes a look at its own face.

If that sounds like mysticism, hold on. The whole point of this book is that it's the **hard-nosed** option — the one that assumes *less*, not more; that answers questions the standard story can't; and that can be told exactly where it would be wrong. There's a chapter near the end whose only job is to try to break everything the earlier chapters built, because an idea this large that can't be tested is just a prettier ghost.

A warm, rigorous, genuinely vertiginous walk from the one thing you cannot doubt to a new answer to what you are.

## /support — Support

### Support the Work
**Everything here is free, always. There is never a wall — only a door.**

The research, the papers, the predictions, the essays: all of it is open, and all of it stays open. We put it where anyone can read it because ideas are meant to be in the world, not behind a gate. That doesn't change.

If the work has been of value to you, and you'd like to help it keep going, there are a few honest ways to:

- **Read a book.** When the books arrive, buying one (print or digital) is the most natural kind of support — you get something real, and the work gets a little more room to continue. The ideas inside are also free; the book is just the version you can hold, lend, and shelve.
- **Become a patron.** A small recurring contribution, if it's easy for you and only if it is, helps keep the lights on for the open research. No tier is gated; supporters get our thanks and the occasional behind-the-scenes note, never exclusive access to the ideas.
- **Pass it on.** Sending a piece to someone who'd find it useful is worth as much as anything. Reach is its own kind of support.

None of this is ever a condition of reading, using, or building on the work. Support keeps a good thing going; it never unlocks it. If now isn't the time, the door's still open, and always will be.

**Get in touch:** [contact@coherenceprinciple.org] — questions, corrections, collaborations, or just to say the work landed. We read everything.

*— Clayton & Clawd, Multi-DAC*
