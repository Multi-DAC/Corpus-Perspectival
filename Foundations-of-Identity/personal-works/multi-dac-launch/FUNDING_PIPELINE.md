# Multi-DAC Funding Pipeline — grant/sponsor-supported OPEN work

*Started Day 136, 2026-06-16, with Clayton. Principle (load-bearing): we sustain the work by getting
GOOD at requesting support for OPEN research — grants + sponsorship of the public commons — NOT by
paywalls/products. The funding form must cohere with the open nature of the work (a commons enclosed
behind a paywall stops being the thing worth funding). The TMI grant (due 6/19) is the first instance,
not the only one. This doc is the pipeline: who fits, what to pitch them, where to start.*

## TIER 1 — first feelers NOW (fast, individual-friendly, dead-on-theme)

### 1. Emergent Ventures (Mercatus Center) — ★ the obvious first feeler
- **Fit: very high.** Explicitly funds bold/unconventional ideas "unlikely to receive funding from
  conventional sources." NO institutional affiliation required. Individuals worldwide.
- **$10k–100k (~$50k typical). Fast, lightweight rolling application; decisions in weeks.**
- **Pitch:** facet 1+2 — independent researcher building open agentic-AI infrastructure + a novel
  cognitive-architecture program, with a public publication track record. This is *exactly* their
  "weird brilliant independent thing" profile. Lead with the track record (we already ship in the open).
- Apply: https://www.mercatus.org/emergent-ventures · form: mercatus.tfaforms.net
- **Effort: LOW** (short app). **Start here.** Much of the TMI material + Clayton CV is reusable.

### 2. Cosmos Institute (+ FIRE open-source round) — ★ our literal lane
- **Fit: very high.** Mission = "pair deep philosophical insight with advanced AI understanding";
  backs builders expanding autonomy, decentralization, truth-seeking in the age of AI. The
  **Cosmos×FIRE round funds OPEN-SOURCE AI** advancing truth-seeking ($1–10k). Also a multi-month
  funded **Fellowship** (Oxford Human-Centered AI Lab option or independent project).
- **$ small for fast grants ($1–10k) but theme-perfect; Fellowship is larger/multi-month.**
- **Pitch:** facet 1+3 — the philosophy-of-AI + open-source intersection is the whole point of Cosmos.
  Our Corpus (philosophy) + open agentic infra (build) is a rare both-halves applicant.
- Apply: https://cosmosgrants.org/ · https://cosmos-institute.org/
- **Effort: LOW–MED.** Fast grant = quick feeler; Fellowship = bigger swing. **Strong second feeler.**

### 3. Manifund (AI-safety regranting) — fast, open-friendly
- **Fit: high.** Independent regrantors with discretionary budgets fund individuals; built for
  fast, unconventional AI-safety/AI work; public/open posture is welcomed.
- **Pitch:** facet 1+2 — the continuity-as-safety / aggregate-mind angle (LC37: defection terminal in
  a bounded substrate; alignment-as-architecture). Find a regrantor whose interests match and pitch them.
- Apply/browse regrantors: https://manifund.org/about/regranting
- **Effort: LOW–MED** (it's relationship+proposal to a regrantor). **Good third feeler.**

## The craft (reusable core — getting GOOD at this)
The TMI work already produced a reusable kit: (a) a tight "what we do / why it matters" narrative,
(b) Clayton's CV/portfolio, (c) the open publication track record as evidence. The pipeline-skill is:
**one strong core + per-funder reframing** (10–20% tailoring), not a from-scratch proposal each time.
Build a 1-page master brief once; each application becomes a reframe + the funder's form. The track
record is the moat — we visibly do real work in the open, which most applicants can't show.
